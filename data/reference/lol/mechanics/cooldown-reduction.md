# Cooldown reduction

> *For the successor stat, see ability haste.*

|   | "You belong in a museum!" | This article or section may contain obsolete information, but exists here for historical purposes. |
| --- | --- | --- |

**Cooldown Reduction** (**CDR**) was a statistic that reduced the cooldown of champion abilities. It did not affect static cooldowns nor on-target cooldowns, unless it was otherwise stated. Other forms of cooldown reduction existed for items, summoner spells, and specifically ultimate abilities.

**Cooldown reduction** was gained by items, runes, and buffs. It was capped at 40%, increased to 45% with Cosmic Insight.

The formula for **cooldown reduction** was: *Base Cooldown × (1 − **CDR** ÷ 100)*

## Trivia

### Highest CDR

***Last updated:** December 15th, 2019 – V9.24*

One cost efficient way to have reached maximum CDR *(45%)* was:

- Having both Cosmic Insight and Transcendence, and reaching level 10, giving **15% CDR**.

- Then by buying the Ionian Boots of Lucidity which will provide **10% CDR**.

- And finally by purchasing a **20% CDR–item**.

  - Frozen Heart – 2700

  - Iceborn Gauntlet – 2700

  - Hextech GLP-800 – 2800

  - Nashor's Tooth – 3000

  - Black Cleaver – 3000

  - Archangel's Staff – 3200

  - Luden's Echo – 3200

  - Essence Reaver – 3300

  - Trinity Force – 3773

The most cooldown reduction any champion could have obtained at level 1 with 500 was **15%**. To achieve this a champion must:

- Have had the Cosmic Insight rune which will provide **5% CDR**.

- Have had the Crest of Insight from the Blue Sentinel (**10% CDR**)

> Relevant mathematics:

> 5% + 10%  = **15%**

**Champion statistics**

 League of Legends content

Howling Abyss Aura

- +70 summoner spell haste.

- Restores  0.1% of **maximum** mana per second.

- Passively gains  5 experience per second.

- Reduces all outgoing heals by 50%. *The healing reduction does not affect self-heals.*

- Omnivamp and  life steal healing effectiveness against minions is reduced by 40%.

- While alive and within 1250 units of an enemy minion that dies, gain 6 from its death (unless you scored the killing blow on it).

- Melee champions gain  15 **bonus** magic resistance and their basic attacks deal 20% increased damage against siege minions and super minions.

- Melee champions deal 20% increased damage against structures, if they adaptively have more ability power. *This buff is replaced by the generic time-based ARAM buff's bonus damage to structures once the latter exceeds 20%.*
