**Atakhan, Bringer of Ruin** was a powerful epic monster in Summoner's Rift that can spawn at either end of the river. His spawn location is determined by the sum of champion combat that has taken place between the top half and the bottom half of the map by 14 minutes.

Atakhan is *Thornbound*: slaying him purges his influence from the map. The slaying team is awarded the Spiritual Purification buff and all Blood Roses on the map automatically. Their Bloody Petals are purified into Spirit Petals, which grant increased effects, and he throws extra Spirit Petals shortly afterward.

## Features

### Spawn

- **Insatiable Destruction:** **Atakhan** demands combat around the map in order to develop his plan for destruction. Each kill and point of damage against enemy champions that takes place on either half of the map—top or bottom—increases an internal score. Score accumulation is different between kills and damage and between the top and bottom half. The following rules apply to score accrual:

  - Generically by game time, damage to champions generates 100% – 0% (based on minutes) **bonus** score.

  - While no allies are nearby, ( 85% / 65%) more score is generated.

  - The first kill generates significantly more score; for each subsequent kill that takes place, this multiplier decreases.

  - Kills against enemies under the range of an allied turret or between allied turrets (of the same lane) grant significantly more points.

- **Summoning Arena:** At **14:00**, the half of the map where a higher score has been accumulated determines **Atakhan's** spawn location. His camp is marked by two slim walls of rose thorns in a circular arrangement and facing each other along the river. The patch of brush on the river within those walls is removed.

- **War Ender:** As a premonitory symbol of his ruin, **Atakhan** causes Blood Rose plants to spawn around the map. This stops after **Atakhan** spawns.

### Passives

- **Debuff Immunity:** **Atakhan** is immune to all crowd control except stasis. His stats cannot be modified by any external means.

- **Persistent Vision:** **Atakhan** is always visible within brush if a champion has line of sight of its location.

- **Frail and Unworthy:** **Atakhan's** attacks and abilities apply stacks of *Frail and Unworthy* to enemies hit for 4 seconds, refreshing on subsequent hits and stacking up to 50 times. During combat, he applies 1 stack to all enemies within his pit every 2 seconds as well. Each stack reduces the affected target's  armor and  magic resist by 1, for a maximum total of 50 resistances reduction. This debuff is also removed upon his death.

### Attacks & Abilities

**Atakhan** will cast his bolt ability 1.5 seconds after starting combat, then his form ability 0.5 seconds after the bolt ability ends. He may perform his basic attacks during these delays. His form ability has a 7.5-second cooldown; during this cooldown, he may only perform basic attacks. **Atakhan** will cast his form ability every time and immediately after its cooldown elapses, and will also always cast his bolt ability 0.5 seconds after his form ability ends.

- **Basic Attack:** **Atakhan** slashes the nearest target, dealing 100% AD (+ 3% of the target's **current** health) physical damage.

  - Each hit applies 1 stack of *Frail and Unworthy*.

- **Bolt Ability:** After a 0.5-second delay, **Atakhan** barrages 4 eldritch bolts over 1.75 seconds in the direction of the nearest target at the time of cast, once every 0.4375 seconds over the duration. Each bolt deals 25.5 – 68 (based on level) (+ 2% of target's **current** health) magic damage to enemies hit, for a maximum total of 102 – 272 (based on level) (+ 2% of target's **current** health for 4 times).

  - Missile range: 800 units

  - Each hit applies 5 stacks of *Frail and Unworthy*.

- **Form Ability:** After a 0.5-second delay, **Atakhan** unleashes 8 constricting rings around himself over 2.75 seconds. Each ring deals 60 – 160 (based on level) (+ 8% of target's **current** health) magic damage to enemies hit.

  - Major ring radius: 900 units

  - Least ring radius: 225 units

  - Ring width: 150 units

  - Each hit applies 5 stacks of *Frail and Unworthy*.

### Notes

- **Atakhan** cannot move nor perform basic attacks while casting his abilities.

- **Atakhan's** abilities cannot be interrupted by stasis.

Cc-Immune

**Crowd control immunity** prevents **any** type of crowd control from being *applied* to the unit. CC which has already been applied will still have an effect on the unit. Epic monsters (excluding a summoned Rift Herald) and turrets are always immune to all crowd control, with the exception of stasis.

Additional effects over the *crowd control* will not be prevented (e.g. Malzahar's Nether Grasp will not suppress but the tether still applies).

Stasis

A unit that is in *stasis* is unable to control its movement, declare attacks, cast abilities, activate items or use **any** summoner spells for the duration, while also being invulnerable and untargetable. ***Stasis* is an immobilizing effect**.

Sight

**Sight** is a property of units, structures, certain summoner spells, items, and abilities that represents a team possessing vision of a target area or unit. It is always center range. A line of vision cannot extend beyond terrain or an unrevealed brush.

**Sight** can also refer to an effect that makes a unit visible regardless of the fog of war or brush, allowing them to be targeted. Standard sight cannot see stealthed units, but True Sight can.

- Nearsight is a crowd control debuff that inhibits **sight**; allied vision is lost in addition to a reduced sight radius.

- Obscured vision is similar to **sight** in a way that it can observe units, but does not render units *targetable*, rather it exposes their position and informs of their presence.

- Unobstructed vision is a form of **sight** that allows a unit to be able to see their full vision radius, ignoring any obstructions that would otherwise restrict visibility.

- All effects that use unit-targeting require **sight** of the target. Delayed *unit-targeted* attacks and spells can cancel if **sight** of the selected target is suddenly lost.

Centered range

**Centered range** is calculated from the **center** of a source unit's hitbox to the **center** of a target unit's hitbox.

- Centered range is used for most unit-targeted abilities.

- It is *less* effective and may seem to have diminished reach against large units.

Adaptive force

**Adaptive Force** is a stat that grants champions either  attack damage or  ability power, depending on the current amount of bonus attack damage and ability power of the champion, as follows:

- **Higher Bonus** AD →  Attack damage

- **Higher** AP →  Ability power

1 point of Adaptive Force provides 0.6 **bonus** AD or 1 AP.

If the **bonus** attack damage and the ability power of the unit are **equal**, the stat granted depends on the **adaptive type** of the champion.

 League of Legends content

Adaptive damage

**Adaptive Damage:** This effect deals either physical or magic damage depending on whether you have more  **bonus** attack damage or  ability power.

- Greater  **bonus** attack damage → Physical damage

- Greater  ability power → Magic damage

If the damage contribution of  AD and  AP are zero or otherwise equal, the damage type depends on the champion's adaptive type.

 League of Legends content

Slow

A unit that is *slowed* has reduced  movement speed for the duration. *Slows* based on a percentage value cannot reduce a target below  110 movement speed. Some *slows* will instead modify the target's movement speed to a static value, which may not be further reduced nor increased.

Only the slow with the highest value is taken into movement speed calculations. All additional slows are ignored until the higher-valued slow wears off. The strength of slows may amplify or decay over their duration. Slows of the same debuff cannot stack and will instead refresh on each application unless otherwise stated.

Negative *slow* values will increase the unit's movement speed instead.
