The **Howling Abyss** is a single-lane gameplay map for League of Legends and serves as the primary map used in the ARAM queue.

Following the 2013 Freljord event, which concluded in V3.6, the **Howling Abyss** became the official map for ARAM, replacing the previous Proving Grounds.

The Howling Abyss announcer can be viewed on the Announcers page.

## Environment

Howling Abyss Map

Above the vast canyon of the Howling Abyss and in a remote location deep within the Freljord stands the **Bridge of Sorrows**, the final bridge leading to the Frostguard Citadel from the outside world—the Citadel being the base of the Frostguard tribe, hidden beneath Freljordian glacier. The Bridge is closely related to the historical conflict of the Three Sisters. The blue (Order) team represents the Avarosan and Winter's Claw tribes of Freljord proper, while the red (Chaos) team represents the Frostguard and their masters, the Watchers. The environment is equal parts icy and gloomy, while the Bridge is adorned by unsteady, rocky statues and monuments built in a bygone era.

## Gameplay

The map has a single lane, and each side has access to their own fountain and shop stall. Each side has one Outer turret, one Inhibitor turret, one inhibitor, two Nexus turrets and a Nexus available, and in this order starting from the middle of the lane.

For the first 15 seconds of the game, base gates will block champions from leaving the base. After the base gates have fallen, frost-gates will appear behind each team's Nexus. These gates can only be traveled from the base toward the middle of the lane and not the opposite way.

- At first, the gates lead toward the front of the allied Outer turret.

- When a team's Outer turret is destroyed, their side's frost-gate relocates to the front of the allied Inhibitor turret.

There are four health relic pads arranged in a line along the lane. They are all located next to the map's close (bottom) wall. Each side has one Outer health relic, located just beyond the Outer turret's reach, and one Inner health relic, located between the Outer and Inhibitor turrets.

## Trivia

- In addition to being the primary map for ARAM, the Howling Abyss has also hosted the Featured Game Modes 'Showdown', 'One for All: Mirror Mode', and 'Legend of the Poro King' (the dedicated summoner spell Poro Toss / Poro Dash eventually led to Mark / Dash's creation for regular use)

- The Guardian's Horn, the Guardian's Hammer, and the Guardian's Orb all used to belong to Gregor (a spectral horn can be seen strapped to his back)

- Running around the map are little horned furball creatures called Poros who can be fed a Poro-Snax (Howling Abyss' trinkets) to make them grow in size.

  - If all ten players feed the same Poro their Poro-Snaxs it will 'explode' into 10 new Poros who will then proceed to run around the map as well.

  - In the past, the Nexus Obelisk and Static Field's passive lightning bolts could unintentionally kill them (now they simply run away in fear!)

- The turrets for each team might be styled after the Iceborn (Blue side) and the Frozen Watchers (Red side) with the game mode itself recreating their battle.

  - The gap between the Red side Nexus and its Obelisk features a silhouette resembling Lissandra (referencing the entrance to the Frostguard Citadel which is ruled over by the Seeker).

  - The Watcher turrets' single eye might be implying they are related to Vel'Koz's Voidborn species. Vel'Koz himself is not a Watcher, but a Void rift in the Freljord is likely.

- The shopkeepers have unique animations for champions exiting their area: Gregor fades back into his frozen corpse and Hermit waves goodbye before retreating to his tent.

- Standing on the fountain does not grant increased health or mana regen.

- The map features many instances of visually destructible terrain:

  - If a champion is killed near one of the giant statues skirting each side of the bridge, the corresponding one will crumble and break.

  - Another bridge in the far background will crumble and fall immediately after any team scores First Blood.

  - Walking past the Red side outer turret will cause a couple of bricks to fall from the crack in the ground nearby.

  - Walking towards the far wall next to the Blue side inhibitor turret will cause the giant pillar nearby to crash downwards.

- Waiting 30 seconds after winning a match used to play a monologue by Lissandra telling the story of the Howling Abyss and the Frostguard Citadel.

**Gregor**

- Gregor is voiced by Joshua Tomar (who also voices Trundle)

- Gregor is the only other person who knows the real history of the Freljord (the other being the Seeker who worked for centuries to erase it and distort it)

  - Gregor doesn't know Lissandra murdered Avarosa nor that she singlehandedly rewrote Freljordian history (he assumes the world is ruled by Iceborn)

### Howling Abyss now on PBE!

By **Richard 'Brackhar' Hough**

Hey Gang,

It's been a while since I first talked to you guys about making ARAM an official gameplay mode, and today I've got something awesome to share with you. For the past few months I've been working with an exceptional team of artists to create a space that's an appropriate setting for both the epic battles that occur in ARAM **and** within the world of Runeterra at large. As a result, we've completely reworked the Proving Grounds and, in effect, created something entirely new.

Now, instead of fighting on a random bridge over a black void, you'll be fighting on the **Howling Abyss**, an ancient landmark in Freljord where the Iceborn finally overthrew their oppressors and sent them tumbling into the chasm below. As you might have guessed from these mysterious journals, there's more (a lot more!) our story team would love to tell you about Freljord - but for now, you'll have to wait.

Anyway, work is still ongoing on the map, and it's yet a few patches away from release but we just wanted to invite you guys in a bit earlier than normal to get your thoughts as we begin to polish it up. The environment team will be actively reading the forum and looking for your feedback, so please let us know what you think!

Additionally I wanted to let you guys know that we are actively working on an official matchmaking queue for ARAM and plan to launch it with the map when it's ready. The queue isn't currently enabled on PBE as there's still a good bit of tech work that needs to be resolved to handle the number of players on the environment, but we're fully committed to giving you guys the best experience that we can. So with that, check out the map and explore what we've created; who knows, maybe you'll even find some surprises!

### New ARAM Feature: Rerolls

By **Richard 'Brackhar' Hough**

Hey gang,

A few weeks ago, I let you know we were working on a matchmaking queue for Howling Abyss, but it wasn't quite ready for testing. Well, with today's PBE patch that's changed! Once PBE comes back up you can try out our new queue and help us test out a new ARAM feature we've added especially for this queue: rerolls.

While the random element is a really fun part of Howling Abyss, we also understand there are times you simply don't want to play a particular champion. That's why we created the reroll feature specifically for this queue. When you're really not in the mood to play your particular champion on Murder Bridge, you can reroll and get a different random character instead.

Here's how it works. As you play matchmade games on Howling Abyss, win or lose, you'll earn points you can save up to purchase a reroll in champion select. You'll earn a base number of points every match, along with some bonus points based on the number of champions you own. While there is a cap on the number of points you can store up, you'll be able to reroll multiple times in a single champion select if you've got the points.

And don't worry about wasting your hard-earned points on queue dodgers. If someone leaves your match at champion select, everyone in the game gets a refund except the player who dodged.

Warp gate

**Warp gates** (or simply **gates**) are map elements that offer transit.
Champions can interact with a gate to briefly channel. Then, they dash to the other side of the gate while untargetable. Each route cannot be taken again for some time after using either side of the gate. Upon arrival, players may choose a direction to dash again a distance away from the gate, though not through terrain.

The channel can be interrupted by taking damage, becoming grounded, or manually by the player, though the travel cannot. Channeling cannot take place while unable to cast abilities or while grounded.

The Temple of Lily and Lotus is located somewhere in Ionia. It is a long-forgotten temple of the Canghapi vastaya. Legends say the Canghapi tribe were amphibious vastaya who worshiped the coming together of earth and water. Lilies symbolized peace and balance to the followers of the temple, while lotuses were associated with enlightenment.

Swarm

**Swarm** is a bullet heaven, 1–4 player PvE game available within League of Legends. Players team up as  Anima Squad fighters to defend humanity and Final City from waves of Primordians. Progress the story to gain access to more Weapons and Upgrades to defeat stronger enemies, unlock new difficulties and fighters, and earn achievements! Each of the four maps ends in an epic Boss Fight.
