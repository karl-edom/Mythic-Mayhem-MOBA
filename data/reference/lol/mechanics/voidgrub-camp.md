**The Voidgrubs** are an epic monster camp on Summoner's Rift. It is located in the baron pit, and consists of three individual ** Voidgrub** monsters. Every 12 seconds in combat, each ** Voidgrub** will spawn a wave of four lesser ** Voidmites**.

The camp spawns only once per game, and despawns permanently at **14:45**, or **14:55** if in combat. The Rift Herald spawns in the same position at **15:00**.

Killing an individual **Voidgrub** awards gold and  experience, as well as a stack of the Touch of the Void buff to all team members. Touch of the Void causes enemy structures to burn for true damage over time, and upon acquiring the third, team members additionally gain the Hunger of the Void buff which summons allied Voidmites to assist in taking down enemy turrets.

## Features

### Spawn group

The **Voidgrub camp** consists of a *spawn group* of 3 **Voidgrubs**.

The takedown of the **Voidgrub camp** is announced to both teams when the entire *spawn group* has been slain. The kill feed includes the slayer of each individual **Voidgrub**, regardless of team. Only the first **Voidgrub** killed counts as an epic monster takedown for the slayer and/or the team. Bonus treat experience for Jungle item users ( Scorchclaw Pup / Gustwalker Hatchling / Mosstomper Seedling) will only be awarded once, assists included. Bel'Veth is special-cased to generate 2 stacks of * Death in Lavender* per individual **Voidgrub** killed, however.

Legend: Alacrity, Legend: Bloodline and Legend: Haste currently give an extra 25 stacks for the first **Voidgrub** (totalling to 125 stacks instead of 100 for an epic monster takedown)^(bug), although the second and third will give the appropriate 25 stacks for a large monster takedown.

### Passives

- **Cull the Weak:** **Voidgrubs** deal 50% **bonus** damage to non-champions.

- **Debuff Immunity:** **Voidgrubs** are immune to all crowd control, except stasis. Additionally, the stats of **Voidgrubs** cannot be modified by any means.

- **Horde:** Upon initiating combat, and for every 12 seconds in combat thereafter, each **Voidgrub** spawns 4 Voidmites to fight alongside them. These smaller monsters decay in health over 6 seconds.

- **Defensive Measures:** Whenever a **Voidgrub** is killed, the remaining alive **Voidgrubs** heal for 30% of their **maximum** health plus 30% of their **missing** health at the time of gaining the heal. After being healed, the **Voidgrubs** will deal true damage to themselves equal to the health they restored from the heal over the next 10 seconds. Any healing in excess of their **maximum** health is converted into  **bonus** health. **Voidgrubs** cannot deal lethal damage to themselves with this effect, and will stop the self true damage if they would do so from their health being less than the damage instance.

- **Voidmite Kill Limitation:** Effects that trigger from unit kills will not do so against pit **Voidmites**.

## Notes

- Without slows, the **Voidgrub** has a movement speed of 350. When its patience runs out its movement speed is increased by 20%, to 419.

- Without slows, the **Voidmite** has a movement speed of 419. When its patience runs out its movement speed is increased by 20%, to 482.

- Upon spawning, the **Voidgrubs** stun all units standing on top of their spawn location for 0.25 seconds.

- Smite **cannot** be used on small monsters.

Cc-Immune

**Crowd control immunity** prevents **any** type of crowd control from being *applied* to the unit. CC which has already been applied will still have an effect on the unit. Epic monsters (excluding a summoned Rift Herald) and turrets are always immune to all crowd control, with the exception of stasis.

Additional effects over the *crowd control* will not be prevented (e.g. Malzahar's Nether Grasp will not suppress but the tether still applies).

Stasis

A unit that is in *stasis* is unable to control its movement, declare attacks, cast abilities, activate items or use **any** summoner spells for the duration, while also being invulnerable and untargetable. ***Stasis* is an immobilizing effect**.

| Interrupts: | Interrupts attack windups (excluding uncancellable windups), all channeled and charged abilities and all dashes and displacements. Units that are being knocked up will be placed on the ground, but aren't knocked down (the *airborne* status is not removed in this case). |

| Reduction: | The **duration** is unaffected by Tenacity. |

Impatience

Monsters lose **patience** over time: while outside of their leash range; if they are unable to find their target (e.g. due to untargetability or stealth); and for non-epic monsters, each time they switch targets.

When **patience** drops to zero, the monster starts to walk back to its neutral position, healing itself and ignoring aggression, for up to 6 seconds. Attacking the monster in this state while it is inside its leash range will replenish some **patience**. If that timer runs out however, the monster will run back to the camp and heal rapidly, causing it to fully reset.

Reactive damage

*Reactive damage* is a damage type that is currently exclusive to Bramble Vest and its upgrade Thornmail.

*Reactive damage* does not interact with most other effects, such as Luden's Echo or Arcane Comet. The main exception is:

- Omnivamp heals for a percentage of damage dealt.

Effects triggered by damage subtypes ( physical, magic, true) will always occur regardless of damage type (e.g. Black Cleaver's Carve stacking on instances of physical damage and Morellonomicon's Grievous Wounds application on instances of magic damage).
