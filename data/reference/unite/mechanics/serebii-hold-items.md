## Hold Items
Hold items are a mainstay of Pokémon and return to Pokémon Unite. As you level up, you will gain access to having the hold items and can eventually hold up to 3 different items. These items provide a variety of different effects and boosts to your Pokémon including stat boosts.
Hold Items are found in a variety of places including the shop and as rewards for levelling up and in Battle Passes.
Hold Items Image
### Upgrading Hold Items
In the Battle Prep area of the game, you have the ability to power up your Hold Items. There, you can exchange the Item Enhancer items, obtained through a myriad of areas including levelling up, purchasing in the shop and more, and get your Hold Items up to the maximum level. The higher the level, the great the effect. You require 2,587 Item Enhancers to max out an item
When each Hold Item hits Rank 10 or 20, its passive skill is powered up.
