## Patches & Updates
Pokémon UNITE is on the Nintendo Switch and are capable of patches and updates to the software. This is done typically automatically when your Switch is in Stand By mode but can be prompted by pressing Start on the Switch's Home Screen and selecting Software Update. Alternatively, the updates may come as small download updates as you boot up the game. These updates offer a variety of changes including bugfixes and more
