## Boost Emblems
Boost Emblems were introduced in July 2022 as a new way of boosting up your Pokémon's stats. These emblems can then be attached to your Pokémon in order to gain the effect. They are obtained through the Energy Rewards with a set chance between each medal and each medal has three strengths; Bronze, Silver and Gold. The better the medal, the better the boost.
You can power up the medals if you have 3 or more of each medal of that species of the same rarity as well, in exchange for Aeos Coins.

### Boost Emblem Colour Boost
Boost Emblems all have a specific colour and, if you attach enough of the same colour of emblem to your Pokémon, you will get an additional boost to your Pokémon, based on that colour.

| Colour | First Boost | Second Boost | Third Boost |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Green | +1% Sp. Atk / Green * 2 | +2% Sp. Atk / Green * 4 | +4% Sp. Atk / Green * 6 |
| Yellow | +4% movement speed when out of combat / Yellow * 3 | +6% movement speed when out of combat / Yellow * 5 | +12% movement speed when out of combat / Yellow * 7 |
| Red | +2% basic attack speed / Red * 3 | +4% basic attack speed / Red * 5 | +8% basic attack speed / Red * 7 |
| Blue | +2% Defense / Blue * 2 | +4% Defense / Blue * 4 | +8% Defense / Blue * 6 |
| White | +1% HP / White * 2 | +2% HP / White * 4 | +4% HP / White * 6 |
| Black | 2% move cooldown reduction / Black * 3 | 4% move cooldown reduction / Black * 5 | 8% move cooldown reduction / Black * 7 |
| Orange | +1% Attack / Orange * 2 | +2% Attack / Orange * 4 | +4% Attack / Orange * 6 |
| Purple | +2% Sp. Def / Purple * 2 | +4% Sp. Def / Purple * 4 | +8% Sp. Def / Purple * 6 |
| Pink | -4% hindrance effect duration / Pink * 3 | -8% hindrance effect duration / Pink * 5 | -16% hindrance effect duration / Pink * 7 |
