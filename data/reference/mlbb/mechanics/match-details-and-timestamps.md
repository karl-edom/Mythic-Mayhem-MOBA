# Match Details & Timestamps

This article refers to the things happening in a match (Outer turret defense shield, Lord Spawn etc.) and the timestamps for each important thing.

## Early Game (0-5 minutes)

### 10 Seconds:

First minions spawn.

### 20 Seconds:

Blue and orange buff spawn.

### ~30 Seconds:

Regular Creeps spawn.

### ~45 Seconds:

Lithowanderer and Side Lane crabs spawn.

### 2 Minutes:

-First Turtle spawns.

-Extra Damage from enemy jungle removed.

### 5 Minutes:

-Jungler's minion penalty removed.

-Outer energy shields on Outer Turrets are destroyed.

-Side lane minion reward bonus removed.

## Mid-Game (5-12 Minutes)

### 8 Minutes:

- First Lord spawns.

-Roamers start sharing gold with Teammates.

### 12 Minutes:

-Lord Enhanced

-Minion movement speed increased.

## Late-Game (13 Minutes +)

### 18 Minutes:

-Lord Evolved

-Death Timer greatly increased.

-Movement speed from Base increased.
