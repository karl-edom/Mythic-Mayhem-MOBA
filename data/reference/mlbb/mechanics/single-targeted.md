# Single-targeted

Single-targeted occur when a player uses the skills or basic attack that's only used to attack single enemy. Example skills are Dance of Blades and Shadow Assault, Onward, The Way of Dragon, Steel Bones, Star Power Lockdown and more. For battle spells, we have Execute, Retribution and Flameshot.
