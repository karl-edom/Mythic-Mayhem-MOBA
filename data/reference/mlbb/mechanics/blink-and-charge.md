# Blink and charge

- This article is about a state of movement. For the specialty, see Hero specialties § Charge. For other uses, see Charge (disambiguation).
> A hero moves from one position to another in a very short time.
Blink and charge is a movement caused by a skill that allows the caster to move quickly to a location or in a specific direction.

Blinks and dashes let the caster travel instantly from point A to point B, or follow a path within a time duration; blink and dashes that follow a path can either teleport or chase to a target, or travels a fixed distance.

Blinks and charges can make the caster vulnerable to attacks and will be unable to attack (casters can still use battle spells). It can be interrupted by caster's or enemy's certain skills.

## Blink

> Penetrable movement. — MLBB Skill Notes
Blink or dash is a movement caused by a skill that allows the caster to move quickly in a specific direction; this movement can pass through obstacles.

## Charge

> Impenetrable movement. — MLBB Skill Notes
Charge is a movement caused by a skill that allows the caster to dash in a specific direction; this movement cannot pass through obstacles.
