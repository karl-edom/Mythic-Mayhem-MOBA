# Kill

A kill is the event of eliminating an enemy unit by reducing their health to zero and killing will cause death. If a unit has death immunity effects (such as Unique Passive - "immortal"), they cannot be killed and therefore, cannot enter the death state that leaves the attacker with no kill count.

## Credit

A credit is the property that acknowledges respective heroes' contributions to an enemy's death. Which heroes are entitled to be the killer or the assister, based on their contribution in an enemy's death. Heroes will earn the respectively credit under the following conditions:

A hero is designated as the killer under the following circumstances:
- They deal the final blow that reduces the enemy's health to zero.
- Alternately, they inflict the latest damage before the enemy hero dies from an uncredited attack.
Additionally, heroes are credited with an assist in an enemy hero's death if they meet any of the following criteria:
- They contribute to the damage dealt to the enemy hero without dealing the final blow.
- They provide support to the killer (excluding summoned units) by shielding them, healing them, or increasing their strength through buffs.
- They weakened the enemy's strength through the application of debuffs.

If a hero dies without an adversary claiming credit, then the hero will be executed. Creeps, minions, turrets, or even the altar (fountain) do not claim credit; therefore, dying to them will result in the hero being executed.

Summoned units (such as pets or clones) that contribute to the death of an enemy will always give credit to their respective owners, resulting in the owner getting a kill or getting an assist. A hero who assists the summoned unit that contributes a kill will not be credited with an assist.
