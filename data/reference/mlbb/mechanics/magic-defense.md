# Magic defense

- For its physical counterpart, see Physical defense.
> MLBB Attribute Guide

Magic Defense (Magic DEF, previously known as Magic Resistance) is a defensive attribute that reduces Magic Damage taken and can be obtained through emblems, items, or hero abilities.

## Default

All heroes start with 15 magic defense or 11.1% magic damage reduction. With the exception of Minotaur, Ruby (10 each) and Lylia (17).

## Notes

### Total Magic Defense

In reducing the Physical Defense, the fixed-value Defense Reduction comes first, then the Percentage DEF Reduction and Magic Penetration and finally the fixed-value PEN. This decrease can be defined as:
$\color{violet}{\text{Total Magic DEF}} = [(\color{violet}{\text{Initial Magic DEF}} - \color{violet}{\text{Flat Magic DEF Reduction}}) \times (1 - \color{violet}{\% \text{ Magic DEF Reduction}} )] \times \color{violet}{(1 - \% \text{ Magic PEN}} ) - \color{violet}{\text{Flat Magic PEN}}$

### Magic Damage Multiplier

Stacking Magic Defense have diminishing returns, for example that is 120 Magic DEF reduces Magic Damage taken from 100% to 50% while having a 240 Magic DEF reduces to 33% Magic DMG only. However, note that in short, the more the Magic Defense, the lesser the Magic Damage Reduction received. The equation showing the effect of Magic DEF to Magic DMG is shown below:

| $\mathrm{\color {Purple}Magic\ Damage\ Multiplier} = \frac{120}{120 + \color{violet}{\text{Total Magic DEF}}}$ |
| --- |

#### Positive Defense

If Magic DEF = 100:

<math>\begin{align}
\mathrm{{\color{Purple}Magic\ Damage\ Multiplier}} & = \frac{120}{120 + 100}\\
& = \frac{120}{220}\\
\mathrm{{\color{Purple} Magic\ Damage\ Multiplier}} & = 0.5455 = \underline{54.55%}
\end{align}</math>

With 100 Magic Defense, the hero will take 54.55% of Magic DMG.

#### Negative Defense

If Magic DEF = -36:

<math>\begin{align}
\mathrm{{\color{Purple}Magic\ Damage\ Multiplier}} & = \frac{120}{120 + (-36)}\\
& = \frac{120}{120 - 36}\\
& = \frac{120}{84}\\
\mathrm{{\color{Purple} Magic\ Damage\ Multiplier}} & = 1.4285 = \underline{142.85%}
\end{align}</math>

With -36 Magic Defense, the hero will take 142.85% of Magic DMG or 42.85% Extra Magic DMG.

##### Examples

- 15 Magic DEF → 88.88%
- -60 Magic DEF → 200%

## Counter

- To reduce enemies' Magic Defense, purchase item that provide Magic Penetration, such as Arcane Boots, Divine Glaive and Genius Wand.
- To ignore enemies' Magic Defense, purchase item that provide True Damage, such as Starlium Scythe and Thunder Belt.
- Use heroes that has Defense Reduction effect addition on their skills, such as Carmilla's Vampire Pact, Harley's Magic Master and Silvanna's Knightess' Resolve.
