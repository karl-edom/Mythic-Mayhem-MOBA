## Spawn Time and Respawning

Turtle spawns at exactly 2:00 mark in the game time. It will reward all heroes of the slaying team with Gold and EXP.

Spawn location and countdown timer for the next Turtle are displayed within 120 seconds after its death.

The first Turtle always spawns next to the EXP lane. There will be no turtle if the last Turtle is slain after 6:00.

The Turtle not slain after 8:00 mark will turn into a Lord if not taking damage for 5 continuous seconds. This can be delayed unti 9:00 mark.

## Benefits

The Turtle gives the following benefits to the slaying team:
- 60/70/80 gold for the first/second/third turtle
- A large amount of EXP that scales with each one slain
- A one-time shield that lasts for 120 seconds.

The one who does the last hit also receives the additional bonuses:
- An instant, substantial heal
- A shield that lasts for 120 seconds and repairs itself after 5 seconds of not taking hero damage. The shielded also gains adaptive damage when it lasts.

## Story

### Sanctum Island

Dragon Turtle is the most powerful thunder beast on Sanctum Island. It uses its shield as the vessel to create the Primal Lightning-seawater reaction and bring the Elemental Lord back to its form. Once the reaction is successfully triggered, the immense energy generated will dissipate Dragon Turtle into sparks and thin drops.

### Imperial Sancuary

Cryoturtles are the slowest energy creatures of the Imperial Sanctuary. As it's recorded in the Warrior's Scripts, Cryoturtles are covered with numerous priceless treasures, including gold and crystals. The most amazing thing about it is that it has signed a mystic contract with the Lord of the Temple of Heroes. The warriors believed that the Cryoturtles overlook the Temple of Heroes from the other side of the lake, which meant they were interlinked in some way. To be specific, they believed that Cryoturtles are the critical keys to awaken the Lord of the Temple.

## Appearance

name: Turtle appeared in Celestial Palace and Western Expanse maps. In Imperial Sanctuary map, it is called name: Cryoturtle, while in Sanctum Island map, it is called name: Dragon Turtle.

## Tips to contest Turtle

- It is possible to contest the Turtle alone, but it will be time consuming and is very likely that enemies will pass by.
  - You should have Retribution ready and some health to tank some turtle damage or enemy's poke.
  - Combine with your abilities to increase Retribution damage range.
- The roamer should rotate to the Turtle area to help. Midlaner and EXP laner should be at least level 4.
- After slaying Turtle, retreat back to lanes to farm if fighting is not possible, or make use of the River surge to perform ganks to further advantages.

## Tips when not contesting Turtle

- It is very risky to contest Turtle if your team is behind in level and gold, as your team will not have ultimates and you will have a lower damage Retribution.
- The turtle will respawn at the same time with enemy's buff, and enemies concentrate around the turtle, make stealing buff much easier.
- The total gold gained from a Turtle is 300/350/400 gold for a team, but you can still level the advantages if your team can kill 2 enemy heroes, destroy a turret or steal buffs.
