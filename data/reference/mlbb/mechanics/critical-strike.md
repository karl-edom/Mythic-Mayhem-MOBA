# Critical strike

A critical strike, critical hit, or simply a crit is a type of damage effect that deals increased damage to a specific target. The damage caused by critical strikes deals twice as much damage by default; this damage is known as critical damage.

A critical strike can occur if:

- the damage dealt, either by basic attack or ability, is capable to deal critical damage
- The damage dealer has at least 1% critical chance (except for guaranteed critical strike)

Generally, critical strikes can be triggered from Basic Attacks, with the exception of certain skills and equipment, notably Balmond's Cyclone Sweep.

## Critical chance

> Chances to deal multiple Damage with Basic Attacks — MLBB Attribute Guide
> Chance to cause [critical damage] with [Basic Attack] (including Physical and Magic). The default is 0. A [critical damage] is guaranteed when it is over 100%. There are skills trigger this effect. For example: Layla's Malefic Bomb and the Typhoon effect of Windtalker. — MLBB Skill Notes

Critical chance or crit rate, expressed as an percentage attribute, is the probability that a critical strike will be occur. The default critical chance is 0%. Unlike other attributes, it is not granted by leveling up, but it can be increased from equipment, emblems and talents, or abilities, which stacks additively.

For example, a hero with a 25% critical chance has a probability to deal one critical strike out of four consecutive attacks.

## Critical damage

> The ratio of Crit Damage to Basic Damage. The default is 200%. — MLBB Skill Notes

Critical damage is the damage caused by critical strikes. It is also an attribute that represents the percentage of damage that a critical strike would cause. The default crit damage is 200%. It can be increased from equipment, emblems, or abilities, which stacks additively. This attribute, however, can be reduced by a hero's own abilities or the enemy's critical damage reduction attribute.

If the damage succesfully crits, the [total damage dealt would be the inital damage of the total critical damage, shown by the formula below:

- ${\rm Total\ Critical\ DMG\ dealt} = DMG\ dealt \times (200% + Extra\ Critical\ DMG)$

critical damage would be multiplied to the total damage dealt pre-damage calculation.

## Guaranteed critical strikes

There are some attacks that deal guaranteed critical strikes regardless of critical chance:
- Fredrinn's Appraiser's Wrath
- Granger's Rhapsody (sixth bullet only)
- Hilda's Power of Wildness (full-stacked)
- Karina's Dance of Blades (enhanced basic attack)
- Leomord The Oath Keeper (enhanced basic attack)
- Mathilda's Ancestral Guidance (enhanced basic attack)
- Moskov's Spear of Quiescence (basic attack pierce through target)
- Yi Sun-shin's Heavenly Vow (enhanced basic attack)

If Golden Staff is equipped by the following heroes, a stack of Endless Strike will not add if attacks deal guaranteed critical strikes.

## Types of damages that can critically strike

### Enhanced Basic Attacks

All Enhanced Basic Attacks can critically strike. The following heroes, on the other hand, have enhanced Basic Attacks that can critically strike but lacks the necessary components to deal a full count critical strike:
- Aldous's Contract: Soul Steal (Base Damage and Extra Damage not included)
- Brody's Abyss Corrosion (Damage scaling with level is not included)
- Clint's Double Shot (Base Damage not included)
- Masha's Wild Power (target's Total HP not included)

Additionally, Gatotkaca's enhanced Basic Attack does not benefit from extra critical damage.

#### Abilities

- Badang's Fist Crack
- Balmond's Cyclone Sweep (Physical Attack bonus only)
- Claude's Battle Side-by-side and Blazing Duet
- Kimmy's Aerial Dominance (Physical Attack and Magic Power bonus only)
- Layla's Malefic Bomb
- Ling's Defiant Sword
- Wanwan's Crossbow of Tang (Physical Attack bonus only)

### Equipment

There are items that give additional damage have the potential to critically strike.
- Windtalker

## Decreasing critical damage

### Critical damage reduction

Only Blade Armor (1960) has a critical damage reduction attribute (also written in title case), which should not be confused with hero-specific critical damage reduction. Upon equipping, the wearer will receive 20% less damage after taking critical damage.

### Hero abilities

- Badang's Fist Crack (-80%, only applied to the skill)
- Lesley's Lethal Shot (-60%)
- Ling's Cloud Walker (-50%)
- Natalia's Assassin Instinct (-50%)

## Trivia

- If a hero critically strikes with 100% critical damage, the critical damage is not considered a critical strike and thus does not trigger critical strike effects.
