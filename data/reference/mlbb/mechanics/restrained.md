# Restrained

> A state that prevents a target from moving out of a specific area.

Restrain or restrained is a crowd control effect that prevents the target from escaping the targeted area, making the movement limited and secluded within it.

## Features

- Prevents from moving out of a specific area
- Ignores resilience
- Can be prevented by control immunity

## Sources of Restrained

- Kaja's Divine Binding (+pulled)
- Silvanna's Imperial Justice (+slowed)

## Trivia

- Kaja's Divine Binding cannot be removed by anti-CC effects, including Purify. However, the effect can be escaped only before Kaja drops his lasso.
