# Wall

Wall is an essential natural element in the game. It is also the main obstacle in game map.

## Mechanics

In certain abilities walls refer to the text obstacles.

Wall blocks walking movement, does not block vision.

Wall differentiates the concept of charge and dash abilities:
- When a charge is blocked by a wall, the charger will stay at place and perform accompanying debuffs on enemies.
- When a dash ends on a wall, it will displace the caster to the nearest valid point on the ground.
- Abilities that allow moving out of map also displace the caster to the nearest valid point upon expiration.

## Notes

In Yin's Domain, the inner edge is considered a wall that increases Novaria's Astral Recall's stacks or pulled by Fanny's cables.

The base of turrets and destroyed turrets are considered wall, regardless of faction, and interact normally with abilities that interact with wall.

## Walls in Brawl

There is no walls with pathable surface, so Ling cannot use his first ability and is unavailable in Brawl.
- Therefore Ling cannot use wall created by Badang and Grock, nor the turret's base, nor Yin's My Turn's wall.

There are however wall edges that other units can make use of.
