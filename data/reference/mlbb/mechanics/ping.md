# Ping

- Not to be confused with the internet strength measured by latency.

Pings are player-relayed alerts that provide gameplay information to the entire team. They can be used to communicate with your team faster than taking the time to type and break up your gameplay flow. Many of these pings are transcribed in team chat.

## Types of Pings

### Common

Attack, Gather and Retreat. These pings are the most important out of any match.

*Bold words are pings you should prioritize.

### Attack Pings (Customized)

Gather and Ambush, Clear up Lanes, I'll take the Buff, Kill the Damage Dealer, Kill the Charge Hero, Push First, Push the lane and prepare to Turret Dive, On My Way, Initiate Team Fight, Wait for me, Split Push, Need Ganking! and Invade the enemy jungle.

### Defend Pings (Customized)

Beware of Ambush, Enemy Missing, Defend the High Ground, Need Assistance!, Farm First, Need assistance in the jungle, Protect the backline and advance, Watch out for enemies attacking our Turrets, Hold out and We Can Win!, Turn on Voice Chat, Follow me!, Spread out and watch for AOE/CC, Protect the backline and don't get surrounded, Skills on cooldown, Not Ready for Team Fights, Watch out for ambush, Watch out for flanking.
