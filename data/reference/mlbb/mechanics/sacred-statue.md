# Sacred Statue

Sacred statue allows players to replace an allied turret's appearance.

Players can select sacred statue in draft pick before the match begins. Sacred statues can also be character-specific; They can only be used when playing the specific Hero the statue was made for.

In the match, you may replace the default turret skin by staying near an active turret. You will see a i, popping up on the left side of the screen beneath the map. Clicking on it will immediately change the appearance of the turret to your currently equipped sacred statue.

- With the introduction of Hero Mastery Codes, multiple heroes have received their own Sacred Statue.
- Sacred statue grants  +5 star-raising bonus points.
- Statue can only be used in Classic, Ranked, and Custom matches.
- Summon CD: 5 minutes.

## How to Obtain

Sacred Statues can be bought from the Shop, limited-time events, or from Starlight Member rewards.

### Shop

These Sacred statue are purchasable by spending 50. Twilight Coins can be collected by completing Hero Mastery tasks.

#### Exclusive

Exclusive statues can only be equipped to the designated heroes.

#### Common

Common statues can be equipped to all heroes.
