# Classic

Classic Mode is the first and earliest game mode in Mobile Legends. All other game modes use the rules in Classic as a base. This mode gives 5v5 and the Mobile Legends experience without worrying about losing your Rank, but your win rate will be affected, both from the hero side and the whole.​​​​ Classic Mode is suitable for beginners who need to familiarize themselves with Mobile Legends games. You can freely choose any hero you have, along with the Weekly Free Heroes and any trial cards you possess. However, you cannot have more than one of the same hero on your team. You can also choose which Hero you like, since there is no banning of Heroes unlike in Ranked.

Note: After you confirm to equip the hero you have chosen, you cannot change to a different hero just like in Ranked matches (Draft Pick), so pick carefully.

Classic Mode is a great place if you want to play more relaxed or hone skills using a new hero. If you had bought a hero and is still unfamiliar as to how the hero works, you can familiarize yourself on using that hero first in Vs. AI or Custom. To find out more game modes, visit the Battle Field Guide.

## Objective

Like all MOBA Games, the primary goal is to destroy the Enemy Base. This requires you to defend your Turrets and Base, and destroy theirs. Each turret in a specific lane must be destroyed in a sequence for your minions to reach the Base. There are ways to destroy turrets by making use of the minions/creeps to take the damage of the turret's bullets. After destroying the turrets, you will be able to damage/destroy the Enemy Base. Destroying the final Turret in a lane will grant Super Minions that inflict high damage to heroes and clear lanes faster. Sometimes, players get caught up with accumulating kills against enemy heroes instead of focusing on enemy minions and turrets. More kills might result in them getting MVP and farming, but destroying turrets, getting to the Enemy Base and destroying it is the main objective.

## Features

- Celestial Palace/Western Expanse — A field drawing inspiration from the Defense of the Ancients: Allstars (DOTA for short) version of the map, with several tweaked features and mechanics. The game consists of five heroes on each team, who are then put in an arena with the objective of destroying each other's Base Inhibitor, resulting in a victory. The general time it takes to complete most games is about 10–15 minutes, but some games can last over 30 minutes.

### Features

- The battlefield is divided into three lanes to fight your way to the enemy base.
- A Jungle populated with Jungle Monsters grants Healing Buffs, Role Buffs, and Gold Buffs when they are defeated. There are also Boss Monsters which provide gold for your whole team or the Lord fighting by your side.
- The Gold Lane can also be taken by Tanks who are roaming for the team to get items quickly to support the team.

## Trivia

- Free Weekly Heroes can only be used in Classic Matches. The free Hero lineup will be announced in the Patch Notes.
- Equipping skins for Heroes will grant them with attribute bonuses.
