# Defense reduction

- Not to be confused with Magic penetration and Physical penetration.
Defense reduction is a debuff effect that directly reduces the enemy's physical defense or magic defense by a certain point or percentage for a while, it can greatly affect the defined damage dealt by your hero and also your allies as well.

## Formula

The formula below can be used interchangeably on finding the total between physical and magic defense. The resulting defense value can go into negative as low as -60, which increases the incoming damage by 200% instead. It scales exponentially. The order of operations can be summarized as:

- % Defense Reduction
- Flat Defense Reduction
- % Defense Penetration
- Flat Defense Penetration
As a formula:
$\begin{align} \text{Total Physical/Magic Defense} = \left[ \text{Initial Defense} \times \left( 1 - \% \text{ DEF Reduction}\right) - \text{Flat Reduction} \right] \\  \times \left( 1 - \% \text{ DEF Penetration} \right) - \text{Flat Penetration} \end{align}$

## Physical Defense Reduction

### Hero abilities

- Bruno's Worldie (4%-8% per stack, up to 3 stacks)
- Chou's Only Fast (10(+1*Hero Level), up to 2 stacks)
- Dyrroth's Spectre Step (40%, 60% if enhanced)
- Irithel's Strafe (15-40)
- Roger's Open Fire (10-30)
- Saber's Enemy's Bane (3-8 per stack, up to 5 stacks)
- Sun's Simian God (5% per stack, up to 6 stacks)
- Zilong's Spear Strike (15-30)

## Magic Defense Reduction

### Hero abilities

- Eudora's Ball Lightning (10-25)
- Harley's Magic Master (2 per stack, up to 10 stacks)
- Johnson's Electromagnetic Waves (10% per stack, up to 3 stacks)
- Nana's Molina Smooch (30%-40%)
- Zetian's Phoenix Descent (10%-20%)

### Equipment

- Genius Wand (3+(0.3*Hero Level), up to 3 stacks)

## Hybrid Defense Reduction

### Hero abilities

- Alucard's Fission Wave (steal 10-20 Hybrid Defense)
- Carmilla's Vampire Pact (steal 7-11 per stack, up to 5 stacks)
- Edith's Primal Wrath (Each point of Extra Physical Defense and Magic Defense into 3.5 Magic Power, self)
- Hilda's Blessing of Wilderness (4% per stack, up to 6 stacks)
- Silvanna's Knightess' Resolve (3-6 per stack, up to 5 stacks)

### Equipment

- Cursed Helmet (3 per stack, up to 3 stacks)
