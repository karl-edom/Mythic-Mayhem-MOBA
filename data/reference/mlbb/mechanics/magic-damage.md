# Magic damage

- This article is about the damage type. For the specialty, see Hero specialties; Magic Damage.
Magic damage is one of three types of damage in Mobile Legends: Bang Bang.

## Statistics of magic damage

Magic damage is mostly dealt by mages through magic skills, and also dealt by magic basic attack, equipment, and talents.

Magic damage triggers Lifesteal if it came from magic skill and basic attacks, and spell vamp if it only came from magic skills.

Magic defense and damage multipliers reduce magic damage, but magic defense's efficacy is also lowered by magic penetration and defense reduction.

## Scaling by Physical Attack

These are heroes' skills that deals magic damage but scales with physical attack.
- Bane's Deadly Catch
- Natan's Superposition and Interference!
- Kimmy's Aerial Dominance, Anti-Grav Thruster and Traction Pulse
- Edith's Primal Wrath basic attacks.
