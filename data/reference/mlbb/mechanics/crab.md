## Description

The Crab gives you a Gold Buff which will provide an extra gold every second for a limited time when defeated. The Little Crab appears first before the Crab. The developers added the Crab in previous versions in order to give players more impetus to compete for jungle control, as well as to reward teamwork. Due to the crab's position being far away from where players usually fight, he soon became largely ignored. In the new map, they have given the crab a new life, as it will now be more closely situated to the top and bottom lanes, they hope this will break up the monotony of the top and bottom laning phases, allowing players use the crab to snowball or to chase a lead.

## Appearance

In the Sanctum Island map, it is called the name: Scavenger Crab and shows more HD in details.

## Story

### Sanctum Island

Scavenger Crabs like everything shiny. They pick up the treasures scattered around the ancient temple and stick them on their shells, turning themselves into moving "treasures".

### Imperial Sanctuary

Shuttling around the riverway of the Imperial Sanctuary, you can find a lot of Crabs there. They are fond of collecting ancient gold and shells there. Hordes of treasure could be found inside their big house. That's why people here like to call them the Imperial Treasure.
