# Attack range

Attack range is the range in which a unit can perform basic attack on enemies. All units in game have certain attack range.

## Mechanics

When a unit is ordered to attack, is not disarmed and a valid unit is in its range, it will perform the basic attack.

When no valid unit is nearby, depending on the setting option, it will either approach a valid target just outside of the range or stay still and perform attack hitting no one.

## Layla's attack range

- Layla has attack range of 4.3/4.9/5.5/6.1 with Destruction Rush at level 0/1/2/3.

- Malefic Bomb further increases range by 1.6/1.4/1.2/1.

- Without Malefic Gun, Layla's attack range can reach 7.1 at level 12. Her range can reach 7.952 with 12% range bonus from Malefic Gun, which is roughly 1.5x turret range.

## Counter

- During laning phase, if your goldlaner has shorter attack range, utilize the bushes and abilities.
- Be mindful when enemies have Enhanced basic attack available.
- For Layla and heroes with Malefic Gun stay close to force them to keep on moving.
- Currently, there are no abilities nor items that limit the attack range.

## Strategy

- In early game, basic attack arrange is mainly important for heroes in gold lane, as it helps them poke enemy goldlaner, secure last hit or trade damage.
  - Lesley, Ixia, Clint, Layla are heroes with high base attack range, allow you to poke enemies and last hit.
  - You may want to be more aggressive with shorter heroes like Harith, Wanwan

- In mid lane, some mages can additionally use their basic attack range to poke enemies, such as Lunox, Kagura, Cecilion,...

- Combined with a gap closer, heroes with very far enhanced basic attack can close up with enemies without having to use Flicker, such as Yu Zhong, Khaleed, Phoveus,...

- In late game, Malefic Gun allows you to deal damage and kite from a safe distance.
  - Many marksmen can attack further than turret with this item.

## Trivia

- Layla is the only hero with damage scales with basic attack range, and has permanent attack range increase with ability.
- In M6 Championship, due to his overwhelming presence, Bruno received a nerf on attack range from 5 >> 4.8.
