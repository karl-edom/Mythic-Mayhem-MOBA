# Untargetability

> A state where a hero can't be locked onto, taking no damage within the duration. — MLBB State Guide
> You are immune to enemy CC and damage effects within the duration. It doesn't remove any existing damage effects or damage over time. — MLBB Skill Notes
Untargetability (also known as Invincibility or Invulnerability) is a state for heroes in which they cannot be locked onto or affected by the enemy's target-lock, auto-lock, and area of effect skills, gaining immunity to all types of damage and negative effects, particularly debuffs and crowd controls for a time.

With a few exceptions, effects that have already been applied, such as crowd controls and debuffs, are not removed or invalidated by untargetability.

Some heroes have direct access to untargetability while all heroes can access it by using Winter Crown.

Note that some allied abilities, and certain enemy abilities persist through Untargetability.
- For example, Aurora who is under her passive Pride of Ice, is still healed by allied Floryn's Bloom.
- Leomord under Winter Crown can still pick up passing Phantom Steed and enter Mounted State.
- An enemy Gloo's Grab, Grab will not be canceled if the host becomes untargetable.
- When an untargetable unit taunts enemies, the enemies still attack and trigger the untargetable unit's passive and equipment passives but do no damage to them.
