# Vs. AI

pagename: Vs. AI (Old)

The "Vs A.I" mode in Mobile Legends: Bang Bang was introduced around late 2017, shortly after the game's global release. Originally featuring only Simple and Normal difficulties, the mode underwent significant upgrades, including a Hard mode added during Project Next in 2020 and modernized, challenging A.I. training modes released around November 2023.

Just as the name of the mode suggests, this game mode will present the player with a 5 vs 5 matchup, similar to any other mode. compete against computer-controlled opponents. An AI (artificial intelligence) team automatically driven by the computer will fight against you.

Usually, the VS A.I Mode is used as a training ground to play or try out a new hero. Since the outcome of a match played in this game mode does not affect your game statistics and you can also earn battle points after the completion of a VS A.I match. As always, players can invite up to 4 friends to practice together against the computer.

## Mechanics

### Game Modes

There are three game modes you can pick in this mode: Classic, Brawl and Draft Pick.

## Difficulty Levels

There are also three difficulty settings: AI: Simple, AI: Normal and AI: Hard.

- AI:Easy has very easy AI, good for practicing a hero you have just unlocked or are not yet familiar with.

- AI:Normal has slightly more advanced AI opponents who can sometimes pose as a threat as they farm, lane, and carefully use skills. This difficulty is a bit more challenging than AI: Simple, and a much better option to practice a hero in an almost-real fight.

- AI:Hard has moderately advanced AI opponents who know when to use their battle spells and properly time their skills to kill their opponent. They also attack turrets correctly unike in normal and simple where they die voluntarily, they also know how to macro, and follow their team, and knows actually how to escape, reccomended to use main hero.
