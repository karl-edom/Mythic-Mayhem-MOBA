# Physical defense

- For its magical counterpart, see Magic defense.
> An attribute to reduce Physical Damage received. — MLBB Attribute Guide

Physical defense (Physical DEF, formerly known as Armor) is an attribute that reduces the incoming amount of physical damage received. All units have base physical defense and 0 extra defense, and it can be increased through emblems, equipment, and hero abilities.

## Notes

### Total physical defense

In reducing the physical defense, the fixed-value defense reduction comes first, then the percentage DEF reduction and physical penetration, and finally the fixed-value penetration. This decrease can be defined as:

${\rm Target\text{'}s\ total\ DEF} = (initial\ DEF - fixed\text{-}value\ DEF\ Reduction) \times (100% - %\ DEF\ Reduction)$

### Physical damage multiplier

(See: Damage calculation)
Stacking physical defense have diminishing returns, for example, that is 120 physical DEF reduces physical damage taken from 100% to 50% while having a 240 physical DEF reduces to 33% physical damage only. However, note that in short, the more the physical defense, the lesser its damage reduction receives.
The equation showing the effect of physical DEF on physical damage is shown below:

$\mathrm{physical\ DMG\ multiplier} = \frac{120} {120 + physical\ DEF}$

#### Positive defense

If physical defense = 100:

<math>\begin{align}
\mathrm{{\color{Red}Physical\ DMG\ multiplier}} & = \frac{120}{120 + 100}\\
& = \frac{120}{220}\\
\mathrm{{\color{Red} Physical\ DMG\ multiplier}} & = 0.5455 = \underline{54.55%}
\end{align}</math>

With 100 physical defense, the hero will take 54.55% of physical damage.

#### Negative defense

If Physical Defense = -36:

<math>\begin{align}
\mathrm{{\color{Red}Physical\ DMG\ multiplier}} & = \frac{120}{120 + (-36)}\\
& = \frac{120}{120 - 36}\\
& = \frac{120}{84}\\
\mathrm{{\color{Red} Physical\ DMG\ multiplier}} & = 1.4285 = \underline{142.85%}
\end{align}</math>

With -36 physical defense, the hero will take 142.85% of physical damage or 42.85% extra physical damage.

##### Examples

- 15 Physical DEF → 88.88%
- -60 Physical DEF → 200%

## Counter

- To counter enemies' Physical Defense, purchase item that provide Physical Penetration, such as Fury Hammer, Malefic Roar, Blade of the Heptaseas and Hunter Strike.
- To ignore enemies' Physical Defense, purchase item that provide True Damage, such as Endless Battle, Thunder Belt and War Axe.
- Use heroes that has Defense Reduction effect addition on their skills, such as Carmilla's Vampire Pact, Dyrroth's Spectre Step and Saber's Enemy's Bane.

## History

- Since Patch 1.5.88, the minimum physical and magic defense is now negative 60, instead of 0.

## Trivia

- The highest known physical defense that can be ever obtained is 1235. This can be done by using Level 15 Khufra with below 40% health equipped with physical defense-oriented Level 60 Tank Emblem, Firmness talent, Tenacity talent, and six Blade Armor while Khufra's maxed leveled Bouncing Ball is active.
