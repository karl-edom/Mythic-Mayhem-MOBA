# Altar

An altar (also known as fountain or spawn), is a type of turret where heroes spawn, respawn after dying, and teleport to after completing Recall. When allied heroes are on the platform, they quickly regain health and mana, while enemy heroes take massive amount of true damage in a short period of time.

The two altars are invulnerable and untargetable to damage.

## Location

Both teams' altars are located on their teams' respective bases behind the inhibitor.

## Effects

The altars in Classic and Ranked games, Altar grants allied heroes healing equal to 10% of their total health and mana restoration equal to 10% of their total mana every 0.2 seconds, and bonus movement speed buff.

If a hero enters their enemies' altar, they will take 2000 true damage in the area of effect every 0.2 seconds (totalling 10000 DPS), this prevents players from farming kills. This damage cannot be reduced and there is no indicator range for altar's damage.

Heroes with death immunity, damage immunity or is untargetable cannot take the altar's true damage effect.

In Brawl, both altars do not grant healing, mana restoration, or increased movement speed. Their true damage area of effect is increased that it extends beyond the barrier.

## Abilities allowing Altar diving

Beware when enemies with these abilities pursue further in the Altar:

Argus's Eternal Evil

Lunox's Power of Order Brilliance
