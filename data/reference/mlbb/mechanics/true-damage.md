# True damage

True damage is one of three types of damage in Mobile Legends: Bang Bang. True damage ignores any damage reduction effects.

## Counters

True damage cannot be directly modified by damage reductions, but it is still modified by pre-damage multiplier effects such as Lancelot's Soul Cutter, Gloo's Stick, Stick, or arcade mode modifications that alter the target's damage dealt. True damage will remain the same despite damage reduction effects such as physical defense/magic defense and damage reduction is on effect, but there are other options to counter it:
- Building health causes the fixed value of true damage to deal a lower percentage of extra damage when compared to max health.
- Shields can still absorb any damage including true damage (excluding true damage that ignores shield effects by Esmeralda's Starmoon Casket or Execute).
