# Resilience

Resilience or control/crowd control reduction is an attribute that reduces the duration of all incoming crowd control effects, except for airborne, knocked back/pulled, and suppressed.

## Mechanics

### Formula

Resilience is inversely proportional to the crowd control duration that the unit has to receive. The formula below shows its relationship of control duration with resilience:
- ${\rm Total\ CC\ duration} = Initial\ CC\ duration \times (100% - Total\ resilience)$
The duration cannot be decreased below 0.1 seconds.

All heroes start with a default 0% Resilience.

## Stacking Resilience

Resilience stacks additively if it comes from the same source (same ability or item).

When a unit has multiple different Resilience sources, the total resilience stacks multiplicatively and is calculated as follow:

$Total Resilience = 1 - (1-Resilience A)*(1- Resilience B)*(1- Resilience C)*...$

- Example:
  - How much resilience does Harith have with Tough Boots, Rock Potion, maxed stack Brute Force Breastplate and his ultimate Zaman Force?
  - Tough Boots gives 30% Resilience.
  - Rock Potion gives 25% Resilience.
  - Brute Force Breastplate gives 15% Resilience at max stack.
  - Zaman Force gives 15% Resilience.
  - $Total Resilience = 1 - (1-0.3)*(1-0.25)*(1-0.15)*(1-0.15)=0.62 = 62%.$
  - Harith gains 62% Resilience in this example.
