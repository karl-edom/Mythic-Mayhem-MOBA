An update for Mobile Legends: Bang Bang has been released on the Original Server for the month of July, year 2026. This includes adjustments, battlefield changes, and in-game events.

The update was released on 8 July 2026, 00:00 server time.

## I. From the Designers

We've noticed that the jungle area changes in the new patch have benefited some Tank Junglers too much. Therefore, we're reducing their jungling speed while boosting some underpicked Exp Lane and Jungling heroes.

## II. Hero Adjustments

The following uses [buff] [nerf] [adjust] to indicate Buff, Nerf, Adjustment, and Changes.
Heroes with major changes in this patch: Aulus [buff], Esmeralda [buff], and Nolan [buff].

Akai [nerf]

- Reduced Akai's jungling speed, but increased his burst damage as compensation.

- Passive [nerf]

- New Effect: Basic Attacks against marked targets remove the mark.
- Extra Damage: 25 (+ 5% Total HP) &#x2192; 35 (+ 6% Total HP)

Argus [buff]

- We've decided to improve Argus's early-to-mid game combat and laning capabilities. The mighty "Fallen Angel" shouldn't be limited to the late game.

- Passive [adjust]

- Demonic Slash Damage: 80 (+ 50% Total Physical Attack) &#x2192; 120 (+ 25% Total Physical Attack)
- Demonic Slash Regen: 100 (+ 10% Total Physical Attack) &#x2192; 140

- Skill 2 [buff]

- Base Damage: 60-180 &#x2192; 120-180

Aulus [buff]

- To reinforce his role as a Fighter reliant on sustained Basic Attacks, we've improved Aulus's sustainability and ability to stay on targets, while slightly reducing his mid-to-late game damage for balance.

- Passive [adjust]

- New Effect: Enhanced Basic Attack reduces the target's Movement Speed by 15% for 1s.
- Enhanced Basic Attack's Base Damage: 135 (+ 20 * Hero Level) &#x2192; 135 (+ 15 * Hero Level)

- Skill 2 [adjust]

- New Effect: The first phase of fan-shaped damage grants 20% Hybrid Lifesteal for 2.5s on hit.
- Removed Effect: The first phase of fan-shaped damage reduces the target's Movement Speed on hit.
- Cooldown: 5s &#x2192; 7s

- Ultimate [buff]

- Slightly reduced the backswing time.

Baxia [nerf]

- Reduced Baxia's damage output and jungling speed.

- Skill 2 [nerf]

- Damage: 140-265 (+ 120% Magic Power) (+ 6% target's Max HP) &#x2192; 120-220 (+ 100% Magic Power) (+ 5% target's Max HP)
- New Effect: Max Damage to Creeps: 350-800 &#x2192; 250-575

Esmeralda [buff]

- Slightly increased Esmeralda's early-to-mid game performance.

- Attributes [buff]

- Base HP: 2464 &#x2192; 2660
- Base Physical Defense: 21 &#x2192; 25

- Skill 1 [buff]

- Cooldown: 8.5s &#x2192; 8s
- Base Shield: 400-900 &#x2192; 500-1000

- Skill 2 [buff]

- Slow Effect: 10% &#x2192; 20%

Eudora [buff]

- Slightly reduced the hero's Mana Cost.

- Skill 1 [buff]

- Mana Cost: 60-90 &#x2192; 40-65

Fredrinn [nerf]

- Slightly reduced Fredrinn's jungle clear speed and early-game durability.

- Attributes [nerf]

- Base HP: 2600 &#x2192; 2500

- Skill 1 [nerf]

- Damage Increase Against Creeps: 200% &#x2192; 170%

Melissa [buff]

- Slightly reduced the hero's Mana Cost.

- Skill 2 [buff]

- Mana Cost: 90 &#x2192; 70

Minsitthar [buff]

- Reduced the Ultimate's early-to-mid game cooldown and increased its late-game damage.

- Ultimate [buff]

- Cooldown: 55-45s &#x2192; 45s at all levels
- Ultimate's Physical Bonus & Attack Effect Ratio: 40% at all levels &#x2192; 30%-50%

Nolan [buff]

- Slightly increased Nolan's sustainability.

- Passive [buff]

- Energy Regen Upon Hitting an Enemy with a Rift: 20 &#x2192; 25

Zhuxin [nerf]

- Slightly reduced Skill 2's cast range.

- Skill 2 [nerf]

- Cast Range: 4.5 &#x2192; 4.2
