# Jungle

pagename: Jungle (Old)

Jungle refers to any area of the map that is not a lane or part of either team's base, including the river that divides it. Junglers rely on killing neutral mobs in the jungle to keep up with their laning teammates in terms of gold and experience. In a standard 5-on-5 game of MLBB, 4 players on a team will be Laners, and 1 player will be a designated Jungler. Jungle Creeps are exclusively showcased in the Imperial Sanctuary Map. Jungle creeps are very strong in the early game and thus a jungler is needed to clear them efficiently. The team shares the jungle from the top and bottom areas. Some heroes are more suitable to Jungling than others, especially Assassin and Fighter, due to their ability to deal a large amount of damage in early game to clear jungle camps and/or to gank lanes quickly in the early game.

Purpose of the jungle is to gain buffs such as Purple Buff (Blue Buff), Orange Buff (Red Buff), Gold Buff, etc. The monsters are scattered throughout the jungle area in order for you to kill in return for gold, exp and various bonuses without interfering with your team's lane minions. At the beginning of the match, the monsters and the Cyclone Eye are spawned at different periods. A jungler with battle spell  and Jungling Boot is significantly faster at jungling than other heroes. There are several types of buffs given by killing specific jungle creeps. These include Purple Buff, Orange Buff, Gold Buff, and Walkie Grass. Killing the Turtle or the Lord will give a certain amount of gold and exp to the side that kills it. The Turtle also gives a shield to the hero and their allies that killed it which increases their Physical Attack and Magic Power. Magic Power is increased more than Physical Attack. When the Lord is killed, it will be summoned from the side that killed it. The Lord will charge down the lane with the least amount of turrets or turret HP and attack anything in its way, while enhancing the minion waves on all three lanes on its wake and has the ability to use a charging skill on the first turret it encounters, dealing true damage and disabling the turret for a few seconds.

## Minimap

Before we start at the jungle, we should first learn the minimap. The colors of the dots represent monsters that grant different buffs. Monsters indicated by Purple Dots grant Purple and Orange Buffs. Monsters at the Yellow Dots grant a Gold or EXP Buff. Monsters at Green Dots grant  a Healing Buff. Turquoise Dots grant the Walkie Grass. Blue Diamonds are Cyclone Eyes. The Purple Paw and the Purple Cross are the Turtle and the Lord, respectively.

## Monster Respawns and Buffs

There are different types of monsters, each gives different amounts of gold, experience, and unique in-game buffs. It is vital to know the basics and advancements about these jungle monsters to be a successful Jungler.

## Creeps

### Common Creeps

There are numerous small monsters or creeps, scattered throughout the jungle. These are really easy to kill and do not give out any buffs and are naturally, good for gold and experience only. These monsters spawn at various times and you can clear these camps often, to obtain more gold and experience.

#### Lithowanderer

(See: Lithowanderer)

This creep won't attack you and gives you the Walkie Grass which grants you extra movement speed and Stone Roamer will appeared moving in the river.

Gold Earned: 92 Gold

#### Horned Lizard

(See: Horned Lizard)

This creep has ranged attacks and arouses through increasing its own defense points when it has low HP.

Gold Earned: 92

#### Fire Beetle

(See: Fire Beetle)

This creep will turn into a little Crammer when killed and can survive up to quarter a minute.

#### Lava Golem

(See: Lava Golem)
|
This creep has no special abilities aside from a Healing Buff.

Gold Earned: 81

#### Scavenger Crab

(See: Crab)

This creep provides you a Gold Buff or Exp Buff depending on the lane. Before the Crab spawns, a small Crab will spawn first, giving a lesser amount of gold and exp and will respawn more often.

Gold Earned: 36 (After killing it)

### Elite Creeps

These monsters spawn after 30 seconds of the game and 2 minutes after they are slain. These monsters grant the killer with exceptional in-game buffs, that are classified into orange and purple ones and have their own specialties.

#### Thunder Fenrir

(See: Thunder Fenrir)

They are duo creeps that give you a special buff called Purple Buff (aka Blue Buff), which grants Cooldown Reduction and reduces Mana/Energy Costs. It also grants you HP for each target you kill, the heal varying with the type of target.

Gold Earned: 22 (Small), 100 (Large)

#### Molten Fiend

(See: Molten Fiend)

This creep gives a special buff called Orange Buff (aka Red Buff), which deals extra true damage and slows enemies when you attack them.

Gold Earned: 120

### Legend Creeps

The Legends creeps are comparatively, more difficult to kill. However, they grant the entire team with gold and EXP boosts, along with other useful effects.

### Turtle

(See: Turtle)

The killer receives the Turtle Buff, which grants the killer with a regenerating shield that gives bonus Physical Attack and Magic Power, while their allies will get Gold Bonus and shield for once time.

#### Lord

(See: Lord)

The most powerful creep that launches multiple attacks to the enemies when provoked. When killed, it greatly enhances minions and associates with killers to push. Summoned Lord can charge at the first turret it encounters, dealing true damage and disabling it.

## Jungling Tips

Jungling is a reference, to when you leave the lane, stay in the jungle to level up and help your teammates by doing occasional surprise attacks during group fights. The basic idea here is that your team will be relying on you for game ganking. You should always be on the move, clearing jungle camps after camp and looking for opportunities to gank. Constantly check the minimap and try to camp at the lane nearest to the current objective to secure it more easily.

As your team's jungler, you must equip   as the battle spell.  allow you to buy the jungle boot which itself is the core item as a jungler. The jungle boot help you deal extra damage and get more EXP and Gold from jungle monster. The creep will take less damage when more than one hero standing near them. You can also use the Retribution spell on enemy to give them whether slow movement speed, burn effects, or reduce their Physical/Magic Damage.

### Jungle Rotation

It is important to know about Jungle Rotation to be effective in Jungling.

You can begin with Purple Buff or Orange Buff first. Many people often choose to begin with the buff that is close to EXP lane, so that when they finish the rotation, they will be at level 4 right next to Gold Lane to gank.
- If you begin with Purple Buff, after clearing the Buff, you need to move to the Red Buff and slay three creeps in that jungle. The Fire Beetle will spawn a smaller beetle, which will guarantee you level 4. It is not advised to attack the Lizard as it is on the opposite side of the rotation.
- If you begin with Red Buff, after clearing the Lava Golem, move to the Purple Buff and slay three creeps in that jungle.
- You may want to save Retribution to secure the Lithowanderer, as it helps you get level 4 faster if it is close to the first buff you cleared.
- Never clear minions in mid lane if your midlaner is not at level 4, as they depend heavily on their ultimate. Never clear minions in gold lane, never clear minions in EXP lanes if there are creeps in jungle, as the minions will give less gold and exp early on.
  - However, before 2:00, your damage to minions will only credit golds and experience to nearby allies.
- Since 5:00, you can help allies by farming minions on any lanes. Your objectives now should be only Orange and Purple Buff, as well as Turtles to optimize gold gain.
- As a jungler, you must at least secure one of three things early on: Buffs, Kills or Turtles. It is best if you can do all threes, but don't fall out all of three.

As a ganker and jungler, it is imminent to never reveal your location. Going near to an opposition lane or using your skills on minions, will reveal your location to the enemy. So, it is always advised, that you should always stay in the shadows and work your business, only from there. Try to anticipate the enemy's movements and plan your attack strategy, accordingly. However, keep an eye on the enemy jungler and keep your team updated about their activities. Even when there are enemy heroes near your tower, the only thing preventing them from taking offense on your tower is the thought that there might be a hidden ganker, who might jump on them, at any moment. So, being stealthy and letting the enemy walk into the trap, is highly necessary and advantageous.

Another task as a jungler, for you, is to slay the monsters and get buffs for both your team and yourself. Slaying the Lord or Turtle can grant epic buffs for the entire team. Target for last hits on the monsters with . It is important for you to have a thorough knowledge of the complete map so that you don't have to spend needless time, exploring and finding monster spawning places. Use the in-game pings to communicate with allies quickly to gank, group up for an objective or to retreat if you cannot help them at the moment.

One last thing you should keep in mind is that, as a jungler, you will level up and farm much faster in the jungle.
