## Description

(See: Stealth)
- If there are no units with a source of enemy vision in the bush when you enter it, you will become conceal and will not be noticed by enemy units after a brief delay of 0.25 second, with a crossed eye above the head and does not appear on enemy's minimap.

- This crossed eye will appear with 0.75 second delay if you are sight by any enemy before entering the bush, regardless if the enemy is visible or not.

- You will be revealed after you deal damage an enemy minion with basic attacks, or damage a nearby enemy hero with basic attack or skill cast for the next 3 seconds even if you are in a bush. When you are affected with the sight effect, though, you will always be revealed until that effect ends. Once you expose yourself or being revealed, you also appear on minimap.
  - It is advised to re-enter the bush after attacking as it refreshes the conceal state faster than staying inside.
  - If you use an ability to damage minions while no enemy heroes are around, you remain concealed and does not appear on minimap.
  - When enemies use abilities hitting you are under conceal state but not having any reveal component, your health bar still appears on their screen, and some of their abilities and emblems will interact accordingly.

## Brawl

There are four bushes in Brawl and function exactly the same as in normal matches.

## Mechanics

- Most abilities that have a delay animation that is centered around the caster, is cast inside a bush while the caster is unsighted by enemies, will have their delay animation as well as the minimap animation (if having any) also unsighted. Such as Yu Zhong's Dragon Tail, Luo Yi's Diversion.
  - However, some abilities, such as Lolita's Noumenon Blast is visible irregardless.
- If a dash skill is performed between two bushes that are very close together, the dasher might remain unsighted even if enemy is right outside. Such as Benedetta's Elapsed Daytime between the bushes that are in three consecutive bushes area in EXP and Gold Lane. This indicates that conceal state provided by bushes is a lingering status.
- Entering a bush and becoming concealed does not disjoint any ongoing projectiles.
  - An allied Estes's Moonlight Immersion will be linked to the bush, informing enemies that someone is inside.
- All traps reveal the entire bush. Such as Selena's Abyssal Trap, Popol and Kupa's Popol's Surprise.

## Bush checking tips

- It is dangerous to walk straight into a bush, as enemies will prepare their crowd control in advance.
- All support heroes have bush checking abilities, such as Angela, Rafaela, Estes, Floryn,...
- Some other heroes have access to direct reveal sources, such as Wanwan's Swallow's Path, Nana's Molina Smooch,...
- Bushes are designed to be very closed together, after you check the first bush, you can check if the crossed eye icon appears instantly when you walk into the bush. If not, it means that someone is waiting for you in the nearest bush, or one of the bushes around.
- If your hero use abilities on a bush with enemies inside, it will put Impure Rage, Brave Smite, Lightning Truncheon on cooldown. If you have Festival of Blood, it will heal you if you are not at full health. In some cases it also triggers Killing Spree if the enemy is low in health. It adds one stack to War Cry.
- Some abilities will react differently. Luo Yi's Dispersion will stop abruptly and have a fan shape, Layla's Malefic Bomb will increase attack range and enter enhanced state or Zhuxin's Lantern Flare will cause her to lose mana.
- In most scenarios, tanks should walk in to check bush, ensures the safety of allies.
- Ideally, your team should have at least a hero with a bush checking ability to gain advantage before teamfight, Lord and Turtle contest.
