# Attack speed

Attack speed is an in-game mechanic which is the speed of the hero's basic attack. Unless otherwise stated, it does not affect skills. The higher Attack Speed a hero has, the quicker they hit their basic attack. Items, emblems, talents and specified skill can increase a hero's Attack Speed.

## Trivia

### General

- The maximum Attack Speed a hero can have is 3.0
- Some Marksmen do not prioritize attack speed such as Kimmy and Granger.
  - This is because Kimmy naturally has the maximum attack speed which is 3.0 and all attack speed effects from items is turned into movement speed bonus.
  - Granger is unique to most Marksmen because he is a more skill-based Marksmen than Basic Attack which means he does not need to rely on Attack Speed much.
- Kimmy, Terizla and Marcel are the only heroes that do not gain attack speed in any way, shape or form.

### Gameplay

- Feather of Heaven is the only Magic item to include attack speed.
  - Heroes like Esmeralda, Aamon, Harith and Silvanna use this item in their builds as they are mages that rely on Basic Attack.

### Counters

To reduce attack speed effect:
- Heroes abilities:
  - Claude's Art of Thievery
  - Hylos' Ring of Punishment
  - Masha's Howl Shock
- Item effects:
  - Chastise Pauldron's "Chastise"
- Emblems and talents:
  - Marksman Emblem's Weakness Finder
