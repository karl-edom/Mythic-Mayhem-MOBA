# Damage reduction

For the closely similar mechanics, see Hybrid Defense.

When a unit has a positive value of damage reduction, it reduces all damage received by a fixed percentage.

The damage a unit takes will be
Effective Damage = Damage before reduction*(1-Reduction percentage)

By default, all heroes have 0% Damage Reduction without any abilities or passives. Damage Reduction sources stacks additively with each other and other Physical and Magical damage reduction without a cap.

All hero-based damage reduction sources do not reduce True Damage.

## Term

Damage Reduction can also be called as GDR, General Damage Reduction. The reverse concept with negative value is damage amplification.

## Disambiguitation

- Although Damage Reduction literally means a percentage reduction to Physical and Magical Damage taken, it does not synonymize with abilities that increase a hero's Physical and Magical Damage by a percentage value.
  - eg. Lukas's Unleash the Beast, Khufra's Bouncing Ball.
- The damage reduction percentage or flat is not displayed.

## Example

- Terizla gains 35% Damage Reduction from Body of Smith. An enemy deals 600 physical damage and 300 magical damage to Terizla. Excluding his current armor and magic defense, how much damage does Terizla effectively take?
  - Terizla will take 600*(1-0.35)=390 Physical damage, then 300*(1-0.35)=195 Magical damage.

- The damage reduction comes into effect after damage calculations and may reduce True damage.
- The damage reduction is not affected by both %Penetration and net Penetration.

### Other Info

- Benedetta is thought to have the highest Damage Reduction of 100% however this is not true as she just gains Damage Immunity.
- Aulus has the longest finite damage reduction which is 5 seconds.
- Terizla is the only hero with permanent percentage damage reduction.
- Baxia is the only hero with permanent net damage reduction.
- Odette before her revamp used to gain 50% damage reduction while Swan Song is active as well but it was later replaced with a large amount of shield after her revamp.

## Counter

- Currently, there is no abilities or items that reduce Damage Reduction.
- Since Damage Reduction does not add armor nor magic defense but a value, it is advised to buy items that gives high damage output so that even if the damage is reduced, the remaining damage is significant.
  - eg. Blade of Despair, Holy Crystal.
- Crowd controls, such as Suppression prevents an enemy from acting when they take less damage, or cancel some of their Damage Reduction sources.

## Tips

- Damage Reduction greatly adds survivability in game much better than emblems and abilities that give Hybrid Defense as it is not reduced by any means, nor restricts you from performing many actions. However the duration of Damage Reduction is very brief or the condition is more specific.
