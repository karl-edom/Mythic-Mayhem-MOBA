# Lifesteal and Spell Vamp

Lifesteal and Spell Vamp are the attributes that restore a hero's HP equal to a percentage when dealing damage to an enemy.

## Lifesteal

> Lifesteal — MLBB Skill Notes
Lifesteal is an attribute that restores HP according to damage dealt with Basic Attacks

It was added in Patch 1.7.94, when Physical lifesteal and Magic lifesteal were unified into Lifesteal.

### Characteristics

- Lifesteal starts at 0%, but is increased by emblems, equipment, or hero skills.
- Lifesteal is limited to basic attacks, but applies to any type of damage.
- Lifesteal's healing is multiplied by a unit's healing received but not by its healing effect.

## Spell Vamp

> Spell Vamp — MLBB Skill Notes
Lifesteal is an attribute that restores HP according to damage dealt with Skills.

### Characteristics

- Spell Vamp starts at 0%, but is increased by emblems, equipment, or hero skills.
- Spell Vamp is limited to skills, but applies to any type of damage.
- Spell Vamp's healing is multiplied by a unit's healing received but not by its healing effect.
- Some skills benefit from 40% to 100% spell vamp for gameplay balance because the more the skill hits opponents, the less it benefits from spell vamp.
