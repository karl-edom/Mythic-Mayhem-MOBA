# Physical damage

Physical damage is one of three types of damage in Mobile Legends: Bang Bang.

## Statistics of physical damage

Physical damage is mostly dealt by physical basic attacks, and also dealt by physical skills, equipment, and talents.

Physical damage triggers Lifesteal if the source is comes from physical basic attacks and spell vamp if comes from physical skills.

Physical defense and damage multipliers reduce physical damage, but physical defense's efficacy is also lowered by physical penetration and defense reduction.

## Dealing physical damage

These are heroes' skills that deals physical damage but scales with magic power.
- Clint's Trapping Recoil
- Popol and Kupa's Popol's Surprise
- X.Borg's Fire Missiles & Last Insanity
