# Gold

This article is for the explanation of Gold. For item gold Prices, see here.

## Introduction

Gold is the only currency in matches. It is used to buy equipment to increase your stats. You can get gold from killing enemy heroes and creeps, Turtle, Lord, Turrets and their outer energy shield, selling equipment and from Roamer's passive.

## Ways to get Gold:

-Creeps: ~50-70 (Regular), ~120 (Buffs), ~90 (Gold Crab)

-Enemy Heroes: 50-400 (Depends on how many times you killed the enemy heroes, or if the enemy has a kill bounty.

-Turrets: ~100-150 (Main), ~120-200 (Outer Energy Shield)

-Turtle: Grants 60 for the entire team, and adds 20 Gold per Turtle spawn.

## Trivia:

The Bargain Hunter talent makes equipment purchasable at 90% of their original price.

The player on the team with the least gold (excluding Roamer) will gain the Roamer's gold Passive until they pass the roamer in gold.
