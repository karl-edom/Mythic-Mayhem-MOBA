# Lane cutting

Lane cutting is a MOBA macro concept in Mobile Legends: Bang Bang. It refers to the practice that a player (mostly EXP Laner) goes behind an enemy turret to clear enemy minions for some benefits. This macro concept is frequently used in high ranked matches or tournaments.

## Terms

The term is also called as Proxy, Cut lane.

## Benefit

When an EXP successfully cuts a lane, they will reap the following benefits:
- An earlier level gain than enemy EXP laner, as the enemy must choose to wait near their turret to defend the land.
  - This is crucial at level 1 and level 4, when players have 1 more ability or ultimate than opponent.
- 15-30 seconds of freedom, as they don't wait for minions to meet each other.
  - This time of freedom can be used to scout enemy jungle camp, rotate mid to create 2v1 situation, help turtle, or rotate mid to clear the minions in midlane.

## Drawbacks

- The enemy laner can reserve their ability in teamfight, gain full gold and experience from minions.
- Might attract enemy's attention

## Low cut

Low cut refers to the cut between Tier 1 turret and Tier 2 turret. This cut is safe since it is close to where minions meet, however, enemy EXP paner can quickly approach. It gives about 25 seconds of freedom after cut.

It is advised to cut at xx:20/xx:50 second mark if you are in Top, and xx:25/xx:55 second mark if you are in Bottom.

## High cut

High cut refers to the cut between Tier 2 turrer and Tier 3/Inhibitor Turret. This cut is risky, since it will go past enemy buff and is very distant to your lane and teammates. It gives you about 35 seconds of freedom after cut.

It is advised to cut at xx:16/xx:46 second mark if you are in top, and xx:18/xx:48 second mark if you are in bottom.

## German cut

German cut refers to the cut inside enemy's base area, between enemy Inhibitor Turrets and Base Turret, this cut is extremely dangerous as you may get hit by Turret's damage, enemies can recall quickly and depending on enemy composition, only heroes with lifesteal should ever perform this cut. It also prevents minions from reaching Midlane, cutting gold and experience of your Midlaner.

## Counter

- When an enemy performs a cut, you may perform similar cut behind their turret.
- When you believe an enemy is likely to cut at level 1, accompany where your minions are cut instead of going to bushes first.

## Recommended heroes

- Heroes with very high mobility and damage to minions before level 4:
  - Benedetta, Yu Zhong, Chou
- Heroes with high sustain to deal with counter cut:
  - Edith, Ruby, Esmeralda.

Likewise, beware when enemy EXP laner with such heroes might perform a cut.

When you believe your hero does not match enemy's, use mobility as your advantage, such as Benedetta vs Phoveus.

## Occasional cut

As all other heroes, you might not perform a dedicated cut, but occasionally, such as after defeating enemy exp laner to force them defend turret after respawning while you recall to base.

Junglers fully gain gold and experience at 5 minute, so they can perform a cut since due to their high mobility.

In whatever situations, avoid dying due to lane cutting, conceal your position until the minion wave approaches and perform a high cut when enemy preempts your low cut. If you are halfway when cutting lane, quit cutting if enemies begin to surround you.
