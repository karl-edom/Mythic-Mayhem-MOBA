# Buff

- Not to be confused with the term "buff" when increasing the power of certain game features via game update.
A buff is any status effect given to a hero or a pet that gives them a useful boost in their performance. The opposite is called a debuff. Buffs can take advantage of almost any attribute of a character, such as health, mana, physical attack, and magic power, but can provide more than statistical change in their case. Most buffs are temporary, but some can be permanent (like 's  which permanently provides increasing scope for his attacks and abilities). Most, but not all, are lost when they die.

Some buffs can stack up to a predefined number of times (such as ’s ) , but most buffs do not stack on their own. This means that repeated buff applications on the same target will not give any higher effect and will only cause a buff duration reset.
