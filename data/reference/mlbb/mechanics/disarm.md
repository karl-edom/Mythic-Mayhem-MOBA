# Disarm

Disarm (v., adj. disarmed) is a type of crowd control effect (CC effect). Disarming a target prevents them from using basic attacks and causes them to drop their weapon; if they attempt to cast a basic attack directly through the attack button, they will be prompted with "Being disarmed, cannot cast basic attack." Retrieving their dropped weapon end the CC duration.

## Features

- Disables basic attacks
- Ignores resilience
- Can be prevented by control immunity
- Can stop its duration immediately by retrieving the dropped weapon
