# Cast types

Cast types refers to how an ability is cast in the game.

Most abilities only have a single cast type, while some have multiple cast types, which result in different effects.

Most abilities cannot be cast while the caster is dead, with the exception of Diggie and Floryn's Lantern of Hope.

## Example

Lunox has five abilities.

- Her first skill Starlight Pulse does not require enemies. It is a non-unit target ability.

- Her second skill Chaos Assault can be shot to an enemy, and the projectile follows the target. However she can also aim to the ground. It is a point/unit target abiliy.

- Her third skill Cosmic Fission requires a point. It is a point target ability.

- Her first ultimate skill Power of Order: Brilliance does not require a target. It is a non-unit target ability.

## Unit target

Abilities of this type must require a valid target to be cast. Attempts to cast on empty ground or no valid target will prompt an error message.
- Aamon's Endless Shards can be cast on any targetable enemy.

When tap casting quickly without chosing target, depending on the setting option, the skill prioritizes nearest valid enemy or ally, or those with lowest HP, or those with lowest HP percentage.

## Point target

Abilities of this type must require a point as the target of the ability.
- e.g. Silvanna's Imperial Justice
Many abilities also use the point to set direction of their skills.
- e.g. Saber's Charge

When tap casting quickly without chosing target point, depending on the setting option, the skill prioritizes target the location of the nearest valid enemy or ally, or those with lowest HP, or those with lowest HP percentage.

## No unit target

Abilities of this type takes effect around the caster or globally.
- e.g. Argus's Eternal Evil
- e.g. Floryn's Bloom

## Tap/Hold

Some abilities will behave differently as if two abilities by tapping or holding.
- Yi Sun-shin's Blood Floods

- Benedetta's Elapsed Daytime's Basic Attack

- Suyou's Blade Surge
- Suyou's Soul Sever
- Suyou's Evil Queller

Some abilities will initiate right upon tapping, make it impossible to hold to gauge range.
- Esmeralda's Falling Starmoon
- Atlas's Fatal Links

Valentina fully copies the behavior.

## Hold/Spin

Some abilities will have their indicator rotate when holding and spinning around the ability icon:
- Khufra's Tyrant's Rage can be spinned to determine the knocking angle.
- Ling's Tempest of Blades can spinned to change the landing position the swords.
- Vale's Wind Blade can be spinned to change the facing direction of the Wind Blades.
- Lukas's Unleash the Beast can be spinned to change the facing direction of the Beast.

Valentina fully copies the behavior.

## Customized unit target selection

Some abilities not only require target a valid unit but also reveals the current health and living status of such units.
- Holding Angela's Heartguard onto a target ally's icon will pan the camera towards them and an indicator is shown above their head, visible to both allies and enemies.
- Tapping Floryn's Lantern of Hope then tapping an ally hero's icon then tapping confirm, the target ally will receive the item regardless of distance of living status.
- Tapping an enemy hero face icon Aldous's Contract Chase Fate will remove the information of other heroes.
- Holding Chip's Why Walk? onto a number selection will pan Chip's camera towards the corresponding Beacon and plays its animation, visible to nearby units. Release on a selection will teleport
