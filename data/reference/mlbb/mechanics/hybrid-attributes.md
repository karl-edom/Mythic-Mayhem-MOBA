# Hybrid attributes

- For its counterpart, see Adaptive attributes.
Hybrid attributes are the attributes that increase two attributes simultaneously.

## Hybrid Attack

> Physical Attack + Magic Power — MLBB Common Terms
Hybrid attack is the combination of physical attack and magic power that determines the output of a basic attack or skill.

## Hybrid Penetration

> Physical Penetration + Magic Penetration — MLBB Common Terms
Hybrid penetration is the combination of physical and magic penetration that increases the effectiveness of physical damage and magic damage, by ignoring the physical defense and magic defense of the target by a fixed value or a percentage.

## Hybrid Defense

> Physical Defense + Magic Defense — MLBB Common Terms
Hybrid defense is the combination of physical and magic defense that reduces the incoming amount of physical and magic damage received.

## Hybrid Lifesteal

Hybrid lifesteal is the combination of Lifesteal and Spell Vamp that restores health equal to a percentage when dealing damage through basic attacks or skills.

It was previously only found in Jungle Emblem's Balance and it grants Physical and Magic Lifesteal. But after Patch Notes 1.7.94, Emblem revamped and lifesteal revamped, it can be found in some hero abilities and equipment.

### Characteristics

- If you buy an equipment with Hybrid Lifesteal, both Lifesteal and Spell Vamp will increase.
- Hybrid lifesteal's healing is multiplied by a unit's healing received but not by its healing effect.
