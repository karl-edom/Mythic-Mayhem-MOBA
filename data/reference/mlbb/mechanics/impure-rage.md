# Impure Rage

> Extra Skill Damage & Restore Mana on Hit — MLBB Emblems & Talents Tips

## General

- Impure Rage is one of the four core talents of Common Emblem.
- It is unlocked when Common Emblem reaches level 20.
- It is highly recommended for the heroes who has high mana demand.

## Effect

- Dealing damage with skills deals 4% of target's Max HP extra Adaptive Damage and restores 2% Mana on hit. (If the hero does not use Mana, restores 1% HP instead. The extra Adaptive Damage dealt to non-hero units is up to 120.
- This effect has a 5 seconds cooldown.

## Mechanics

- Impure Rage works on multiple units, whether it's heroes, minions or even jungle creeps.
- When hitting multiple targets, this effect only trigger on one target.

## Tips

- You can safely check bush by casting a skill with Impure Rage on. Enemies inside will either heal you, and put Impure Rage on cooldown.
- Many heroes can equip with both Impure Rage and Lethal Ignition. Lethal Ignition deals more damage but has higher cooldown, and no can't check the bush.
  - Since this talent is mainly of utility type, it is not recommended for Assassins to use. For burst type talent, choose Lethal Ignition instead.

## Recommended Heroes

- Tanks: Alice, Esmeralda, Uranus
- Fighters: Alpha, Ruby, Terizla
- Mages: Cecilion, Valir, Zhuxin
- Marksmen: Clint, Granger, Roger
- Supports: Angela, Diggie, Floryn
