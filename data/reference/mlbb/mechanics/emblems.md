# Emblems

- For the predecessor, see Emblems (2016–2023).

Emblems are hero enhancements that grant additional buffs or effects as well as bonus attributes. In the preparation tab or during the hero pick phase, players can choose their preferred emblem sets. Currently, there are 7 types of emblems, each with its own set of bonuses.

Standard talents and core talents are the two of talents. Players can choose additional effects for their chosen hero via talents, providing them with an advantage with the suitable configurations; however, they can only choose two standard talents and one core talent. Currently, there are 16 types of standard talents and 8 types of core talents, each with its own set of bonuses.

## Trivia

Most of the standard talents and core talents are from the old emblem systems but with a bit different:
- Thrill was one of the former subtalent in Jungle Emblem.
  - It is also the combination of:
    - Bravery in Physical Emblem, Assassin Emblem, Fighter Emblem and Marksman Emblem.
    - Flow in Magic Emblem and Mage Emblem.
    - Catastrophe in Mage Emblem.
- Swift was one of the former subtalent in Physical Emblem, Jungle Emblem, Fighter Emblem and Marksman Emblem.
- Vitality was one of the former subtalent in Physical Emblem, Magic Emblem, Tank Emblem and Support Emblem.
  - It is also the former subtalent - Persistence in Fighter Emblem.
- Rupture was one of the former subtalent in Support Emblem.
  - It is also the combination of:
    - Invasion in Assassin Emblem and Fighter Emblem.
    - Observation in Magic Emblem and Mage Emblem.
- Inspire was one of the former subtalent in Tank Emblem.
  - It is also the former subtalent - Desire in Magic Emblem but without Magic Lifesteal.
  - It is also the former subtalent - Mastery in Assassin Emblem, Mage Emblem and Support Emblem.
- Firmness is the combination of:
  - Firmness and Shield in Physical Emblem, Tank Emblem and Fighter Emblem.
  - Fortress and Purity in Tank Emblem.
- Agility was one of the former subtalent in Assassin Emblem, Mage Emblem, Marksman Emblem and Support Emblem.
- Fatal is the combination of:
  - Fatal in Physical Emblem, Assassin Emblem and Marksman Emblem.
  - Doom in Marksman Emblem.
- Wilderness Blessing was available on Advanced Server for a while and was later removed. Until Patch Notes 1.8.20 returns and replaces Life Drain.
- Seasoned Hunter formerly known as Demon Slayer and it was the former third talent in Jungle Emblem.
- Tenacity was the former first talent in Tank Emblem.
- Master Assassin formerly known as High and Dry and it was the former second talent in Assassin Emblem.
- Bargain Hunter formerly known as Mystery Shop and it was the former first talent in Mage Emblem.
- Festival of Blood was the former second talent in Fighter Emblem.
- Pull Yourself Together was the former third talent in Support Emblem.
- Weapon Master was the former first talent in Marksman Emblem.
- Impure Rage was the former third talent in Mage Emblem.
- Quantum Charge formerly known as Electro Flash and it was the former second talent in Marksman Emblem.
- Temporal Reign was available on Advanced Server for a while and was later removed. Until Patch Notes 1.8.92 returns along with War Cry.
- Concussive Blast was the former third talent in Tank Emblem.
- Killing Spree was the former third talent in Assassin Emblem.
- Lethal Ignition formerly known as Magic Worship and it was the second talent in Mage Emblem.
- Brave Smite was the former second talent in Tank Emblem.
- Focusing Mark was the former first talent in Support Emblem.
- Weakness Finderwas the former third talent in Marksman Emblem.
