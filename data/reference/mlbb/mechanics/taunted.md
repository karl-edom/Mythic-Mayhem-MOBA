# Taunted

> A state that forces a target to do Basic Attack to the caster. The target can't use skills within the duration and will attack the caster again.

Taunt is a type of crowd control effect that forces affected targets to attack and follow the target who taunted them and use. Taunted targets use Basic Attacks against for those who taunt them.

## Features

- Forces affected units to attack and follow the one who taunted them
- Interrupts skills (excluding skills that require channeling)
- Can be reduced time controlled by resilience
- Can be prevented by control immunity

## Sources of Taunted

- Belerick's Ancient Seed & Wrath of Dryad
- Gatotkaca's Unbreakable
- Fredrinn's Energy Eruption
