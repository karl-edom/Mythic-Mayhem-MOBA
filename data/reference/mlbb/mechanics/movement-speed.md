# Movement speed

Movement speed (also written in title case) is an attribute that measures the rate at which a unit can move over a second. All units have a base movement speed and 0 extra movement speed, which can be increased or decreased by abilities speedup and slowdown effects.

When a unit moves at x speed, it moves a distance of $x/100$ per second.
- eg. Suyou moves 2.65 unit per second with the speed of 265.

## Base movement speed

See List of heroes by movement speed.

## Calculations

${\rm Calculated\ Movement\ Speed} = Basic\ Movement\ Speed \times (100% + All\ Speedup\ Effect% - All\ Slowdown\ Effect%)$

The amount of movement speed obtained from speed up and slow sources stack multiplicatively with each other has no upper limit, but the effectiveness of both the speed up and slowdown effects is reduced by an unknown rate when it falls below 230 and rises above 420.
The current movement speed is updated on the hero' statistics.

A unit can be slowed down up to 90% of their total movement speed.
