# Ability tag

Ability tag is a tag that is assigned to each ability in game.

## Mechanics

Each ability has at least one and up to two tags that convey the core feature of such ability.

Depending on the tag on a hero's ability, a hero's durability, offense or control effect stat vary acccordingly.

Certain abilities have their interaction based on these tags.

## Tag

The following tag exists:
- Mobility: The ability contains dash, blink, charge, teleport over short distance.
- AoE: Deal damage and affect multiple enemies at once.
- Buff: Grant some bonus, such as bonus damage, CC Immune, Speed bonus.
- Burst: Deal massive damage within a short time frame.
- Slow
- Invincible: Provide the caster lasting invincible state.
- Damage: Dealing damage
- Heal
- Camouflage
- CC: Apply crowd control effects on affected enemies.
- Remove CC: Remove debuffs on self.
- Teleport: Move the caster (and other units) accross vast distant. Does not count as Mobility skills.
- Morph: The caster turns into a different version with unique set of abilities.
- Death Immunity: The caster cannot die from damage or any abilities for the duration of the ability.
