## Mechanics

- Unlike the Turtle, when Lord attacks his enemies or uses ability, the animation in the Lord Pit is visible to everyone even with no sight.

### Spawn Time and Interval

The first Lord does not have a fixed time on his first respawn, and will appear exactly at 2 minutes after the last Turtle is slain.
- For example, if the last turtle is slain at 7:45 mark, the Lord must appear at 9:45 mark.

If the last Turtle is not slain at 8:00 mark, and does not take any damage within continuous 5 seconds until 9:00 mark, then the Lord will replace the Turtle's position after 5 seconds.

When the Lord is slain between 8:00-18:00 mark, the Lord respawn every 180 seconds after the last slain, reducing to every 120 seconds after 18:00 mark.

When the Lord is slain, his a roar audible to everyone and an announcement is made.

Each Lord slain for a side is recorded on the scoreboard.

### Properties

He is more resilient and has more attack skills than Turtle, with his basic attack using his axe to hit the enemies, and it includes a bit of AoE where the direct impact is not imminent.

The Lord only evolves or awakens at 18 minute mark.

The Summoned Lord gains 15%-35% damage reduction for each nearby ally hero. (Each hero increases the base amount by 4%).

The Summoned Lord at 8-18 minute mark deals 30% Max HP as True damage to the turret by his charge.

### Abilities

Lord's damage does not affect the Lithowanderer in any way.

Thunder Strike: Lord strikes all enemies around with lightning, dealing True Damage.
- The first Lord before 12 minute mark only casts this ability each time he successfully launches 3 basic attacks without having his health reset.
- The second Lord casts this ability immediately on being attacked.
- The animation is visible to everyone even without sight.

Torrent: Lord slams his trident to the ground, marks all enemies' location. After a brief delay, each location erupts a torrent knocking enemies airborne and deals damage.
- The first Lord before 12 minute mark only cast this ability after a Lightning cast and 4 basic attacks.
- The Lord after 14 minute mark casts this ability immediately upon being attacked.
- The airborne locations are only visible to nearby enemies.

### Benefits

The team which defeats the Lord will gain the following benefits:
- Lord will respawn at the next minion wave after being defeated, right after the Siege minion. If the Lord is slain between xx:10-xx:40 time mark, he will appear in xx:43 mark, if slain between xx:40-xx:10 time mark, he will appear in xx:10 time mark.
  - Lord will go on the lane that has the least turret to reach the base. If multiple lanes have the same number of turrets, he will go on the lane with the outer turret with lowest health, otherwise, he will prioritize Midlane as the shortest distance.

- Greatly increase the attributes of the next minion waves of all lanes the team until he is defeated again by enemy teams.

- When sighting an enemy turret, Lord charges up, dealing True Damage to the turret and enemies on the path.

- The summoned Lord is considered an ally hero. Enemy Turrets and Base will prioritize attacking Lord among all other minions. The Turret shield does not damage nor knockback the Lord.

- Allows nearby allied heroes deal 250 (+50% Total Physical ATK) (+50% Total Magic Power) (+2.5% Total HP) extra Magic Damage on their attacks with a Cooldown of 2s.

## Appearance

In Sanctum Island map, Lord shows up as a water-elemental creature and bring the trident as a weapon. His appearance in Celestial Map and Western Exspanse is similar to 's Dark Guardian skin but with a dark helmet on. In Imperial Sanctuary map, Lord looks more like giant lava-theme devil and what seems to be steel or iron armor, lava horns on its head, and yellow eyes. It's shown wearing armor and its chest has flowing lava on it. Lord does not seem to have armor on the lower parts of his arms. His appearance is very similar to ' model, Centaur.

## Story

### Sanctum Island

The Ancient Ones created Elemental Lord by infusing Primal Lightning into seawater. The latter had since served as the guard of Sanctum Island, watching over the thunder beasts and imprisoned Evil Gods. After the Ancient Ones disappeared, it lost self-consciousness and could barely maintain its form. It remains on the spot, waiting for its master's return.

### Imperial Sanctuary

Legend has it that there is a mysterious creature hidden in the ancient Imperial Sanctuary, symbolizing power, victory, and destruction. The Lord of the Temple of Heroes, possesses the supreme power to endow everything on the battlefield with strength and make himself literally immortal. No one can be compared to him and even the bravest of fighters could only seek shelter from him, for a fairly short period. For a long time, the Lord of the Temple of Heroes posted here. Once awaken, he would be the most steadfast defense within the Imperial Sanctuary.

## Tips on securing Lord

- A hero or two should only consider contesting Lord alone if they have high attack speed or abilities that deal massive damage to Lord, or have lifesteal abilities to heal back the massive True Damage. A team securing Lord is much faster and safer.
- Make sure to check bushes, as the Lord area consists of the most number of bushes in the game, and an airbone from Lord can enable enemies to steal.
- It is best to slay Lord after your team wins a teamfight and 2-3 enemies are awaiting respawn, or enemy jungle's retribution is on cooldown.
- For the jungler, it is best to ping allies around the remaining cooldown of your Retribution and use additional abilities while casting Retribution to secure Lord.
- Be careful when there is a Xavier,Moskov,Zetian,Balmond,Suyou or a Helcurt on the enemy team as they can easily steal the Lord.

## Tips on pushing with Lord

- The Lord will not be summoned right away, but take around 3-33 seconds of delay depending on the next minion wave. Ping your team to clear up minions on other lanes and avoid fighting unnecessary to maintain the number advantage that Lord provided.
- Your team can either focus on one or two lanes that Lord does not push, as enemies must assign one or two heroes to halt the Lord. Or you can also accompany the Lord to deter their attempt to halt and increase the Lord's strength.
- After the summoned Lord's death, the enhanced minion wave can still be useful to push turrets.

## Tips on defending Lord

- When your team lose Lord to the enemy, it is best to avoid teamfight and stick together at base to defend Lord. As further casualty will increase the number advantage of the enemy.
- Early lords are weak and when you are sure that your teamn cannot secure Lord, you can sneak push another lane, destroy a turret, steal a buff to reduce the pressure of enhanced minion waves using the delay time before the Lord is summoned.
- Keep in mind that even after the summoned Lord is defeated, the enhanced minion waves alone can destroy several turrets, so keep an eye on them and assign people accordingly.
- It is best to hold off the Lord near the turret, but not in its charge range, so that you will have your back covered and not ambushed in all direction. Be very careful when you deplete your ultimates or all skills to block Lord which will make enemy's gank easier.
- During the 3 minutes interval time when the Lord is respawning, avoid teamfight with clear disadvantages before Lord fight, or create situations and vision that is suitable for the next Lord contest.
- The Lord since 18 minute is the most evolved Lord, and defending it require at least 2 heroes. An ally tank or semi-tank fighter should lure it to the side before it seeing the Base turret.
