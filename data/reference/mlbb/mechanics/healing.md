# Healing

**Infobox system**

| Field | Value |

Healing is the act of replenishing a unit's health points by a variety of methods, including regen effects and lifesteal.

Healing can be replenished in two different ways: through normal healing and lifesteal. Both healing and lifesteal can be increased, for an example: those who have Oracle will benefit from Holy Healing and spell vamp's increased healing.

## Lifesteal healing

Additionally, lifesteal has a healing effect that is a percentage of the type of damage that spell vamp, magical lifesteal, or physical lifesteal causes.
