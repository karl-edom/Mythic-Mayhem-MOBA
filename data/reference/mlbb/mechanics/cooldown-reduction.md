# Cooldown reduction

Cooldown reduction (CD reduction, CDR) is an attribute for reducing cooldown of abilities.

All heroes start with a default 0% cooldown reduction at level 1. By increasing cooldown reduction, skills can be cast more frequently.

It is noted that cooldown reduction is applied separately for items, battle spells and abilities. That means items that provide CDR to abilities may not affect items and battle spells at all and vice versa. So you must check if CDR provided is applied to which.

The cooldown reduction for ability is normally capped at 40%. However it is possible to lift the cap.

When an ability is reduced in cooldown, its starting cooldown will be at a faster time.

Abilities with charges, such as Luo Yi's Dispersion is fully affected by cooldown reduction.

## Increasing Max Cooldown Reduction

Although the 40% cooldown reduction limit can be increased, it is only extended by 5% Enchanted Talisman's Unique Passive - "Magic Mastery".

## Calculation

Cooldown reduction stacks additively to each other.

Example 1: How much cooldown reduction will Luo Yi have with her lv3 Diversion, Purple Buff and Lightning Truncheon?
- Lv3 Diversion gives 10% CDR.
- Purple Buff gives 10% CDR.
- Lightning Truncheon gives 10% CDR.
- So Luo Yi will gain 30% CDR in this example.

Example 2: How much cooldown reduction will Valentina have with her Magic Shoes, Inspire, Enchanted Talisman, Fleeting Time?
- Magic Shoes gives 10% CDR.
- Inspire gives 5% CDR.
- Enchanted Talisman gives 20% CDR.
- Fleeting Time gives 15% CDR.
- Since capped CDR is 45%, she will gain 45% CDR in this example, 5% CDR is wasted.

## Cooldown Speed

Cooldown speed means an ability will finish its cooldown in a shorter real time, while the cooldown of the ability does not change.
- All abilities have 100% cooldown speed by default.

Example: Helcurt's Hidden Terror has the cooldown of 12 seconds and 300% cooldown speed, how much does it take in real time for Hidden Terror to finish its cooldown?
- Hidden Terror would take 12/3.00= 4 (seconds) in real time to finish 12 seconds cooldown.

| Hero | Ability | Speed | Note |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Helcurt | Hidden Terror | 300% | Prowler state |
| All heroes | Non-Ultimate skills | 160% | Temporal Reign |

## Exempted Abilities

All passives of items, emblems and heroes cannot be reduced by cooldown reduction and cooldown speed.
  - Molina's Gift, Immortality, Pride of Ice,...

## Tips

- Most heroes should have some cooldown reduction in late game, pair with the low cooldown abilities to use them multiple time in a skirmish.
- Most supports have very high cooldown ultimates, such as Angela's Heartguard with 70 seconds cooldown at all levels. With 40% CDR, this ultimate can be reduced to less than 42 seconds, allow for more frequent use in late game.
- Try to cap out CDR at no further than 45%, so that you won't waste any gold on items or emblems that give excess cooldown reduction while lacking damage output or healing.
- Some heroes should consider CDR as a minor attribute, as their abilities rely on damage or are already at low cooldown. Such as Phoveus, Lukas, Suyou. Prioritizing CDR items and emblems will be at the cost of damage output for them in early game.
