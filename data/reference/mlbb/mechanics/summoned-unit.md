# Summoned unit

A summoned unit is a non-player character unit summoned during combat and primarily controlled by artificial intelligence.

For targeting purposes, summoned units are sometimes considered a hero and a creep. The owner is still credited for the damage it deals. A clone may inherit nearly all of the owner's attributes and, in some cases, equipment passives.

## Pets

Pets assist their summoner in battle by being instructed to attack.
- Deployed Lord
- Nightmaric Spawn (Zhask's nightmaric spawn)
- Kupa (Popol's wolf companion)
- Eternal Guard(Vexana's eternal guard)

## Clone

A clone is a copy of a hero who is considered a hero for targeting purposes.
- Atlas' mecha (Perfect Match)
- Guinevere's illusion (Spatial Migration)
- Hanzo's real body (Kinjutsu: Pinnacle Ninja)
- Sun's doppelgänger (Endless Variety, Swift Exchange, and Clone Techniques)
- Old:
- Vexana's puppet (Cursed Oath)
