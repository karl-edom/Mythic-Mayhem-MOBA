# Physical penetration

- Not to be confused with Defense reduction. For its magical counterpart, see Magic penetration.
>

Physical penetration (Physical PEN) is an attribute that increases the effectiveness of physical damage, by ignoring the physical defense of the target by a fixed value or a percentage. Physical penetration starts at 0 but can be obtained through emblems, items, or even hero abilities.

While physical penetration only affects the user's physical damage, Fixed-value physical penetration can ignore the target's physical defense below 0 (up to negative 60), causing the physical damage dealt to increase instead. See the formula below or read more.

| $\mathrm{{\color{Maroon}Total\ Physical\ Defense}}[({\color{Maroon} Physical\ DEF} - {\color{CarnationPink} Physical\ DEF\ Reduction}) \times (100% - {\color{CarnationPink} Percentage\ Physical\ DEF\ Reduction} - {\color{CarnationPink} Percentage\ Physical\ PEN})] - {\color{CarnationPink}Fixed \text{-} value\ Physical\ PEN}$ |
| --- |
