# Mobility

Mobility is an Ability tag of abilities in game.

## Mechanics

- Abilities with Mobility tag allow the caster to change their position swiftly in battlefields.

- Some abilities don't have mobility tag in their description, but is treated as a mobility ability, such as Tsunami Slam.

- Some abilities only have a partial mobility component.
- eg. Evil Queller, Heartguard.

- Abilities that grant bonus movement speed, such as Supreme Warrior or Sprint are not counted as Mobility skills.

- Certain heroes have their abilities interact with Mobility tag: Khufra, Minsitthar, Lancelot, Phoveus.

## Bouncing Ball interaction

- Khufra's Bouncing Ball will block and knock airborne enemies moving under the effect of Mobility tag that is dash, charge or blink.
  - eg. Gauge, Puncture

- Mobility passives are also blocked.
  - eg. Tiger Pace, Let's Dance!

- Mobility abilities that cannot be interrupted, or grant CC immune is unaffected.
  - Eg. Traceless, Sword Spike

- Effects that come with the dash fully take place, except the dash.
  - Eg. Power of Chaos Darkening, Primal Wrath

- Teleport abilities are unaffected.
  - Abyss Walker, Flicker

Example: Kagura's Rasho Umbrella Flee's dash is blocked, but the teleportation is not.

## Demonic Force interaction

- All mobility skills, including dash, charge, blink, teleportation, crowd controls from enemies and Cyclone Eye triggers Phoveus's Demonic Force.
  - An enemy from further than 8 unit using a mobility skill into a point within 8 unit also triggers Demonic Force.

- An ability with multiple dashes in one cast, such as Phantom Slash or Surge of Life, each dash triggers Demonic Force once.

- A unit returning from Chip's Shortcut's Connecting Portal does not trigger.

## King's Calling interaction

See root.
All mobility skills are disabled and cannot be cast.

## Soul Cutter interaction

Only count mobility skills from Lancelot, allied Guiding Wind, Flicker and Cyclone Eye.

## Other

- Beside a hero's skill set, Flicker and Cyclone Eye are Mobility skills.

## Trivia

- Benedetta is the only hero with all dash skills from her passive to ultimate.
