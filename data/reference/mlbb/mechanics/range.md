# Range

## Ranged Attacks in Mobile Legends: Bang Bang (MLBB)

Ranged attacks in Mobile Legends: Bang Bang (MLBB) are those launched from a distance, without requiring the hero to be in close proximity to the enemy. This distinguishes them from melee attacks, which necessitate close-range engagement.

For the statistical basic attack range of units, see Basic attack range.

### Types of Ranged Attacks

| Type | Description | Example Heroes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Basic Ranged Attacks | Standard ranged attacks, usually launched from a hero's primary weapon. | Marksman, Mage, Assassin |
| Skill-Based Ranged Attacks | Ranged attacks triggered by specific skills or abilities. | Mage, Marksman, Assassin, Support, Tank |
| Projectile-Based Ranged Attacks | Attacks involving the launching of projectiles, such as arrows, bullets, or energy blasts. | Marksman, Mage, Assassin |
| Area-of-Effect (AoE) Ranged Attacks | Attacks affecting multiple targets within a specified area. | Mage, Support, Tank |
| Targeted Ranged Attacks | Attacks directed at a specific target. | Marksman, Mage, Assassin |
| Indirect Ranged Attacks | Attacks launched through intermediaries, such as bouncing off walls or reflecting off enemy abilities. | Assassin, Support |

### Advantages and Disadvantages of Ranged Attacks

Advantages:

- Safety: Reduces the risk of being hit in return.
- Versatility: Can be used for harassment, clearing minions, and dealing damage to multiple targets.
- Long-Range Engagement: Provides an advantage in team fights and objectives.
- Crowd Control: Many ranged abilities can control enemy movements or disable abilities.
- Objective Control: Can effectively defend turrets and other objectives from a distance.

Disadvantages:

- Vulnerability to Melee Heroes: Can be vulnerable to close-range engagement.
- Limited Mobility: Some ranged heroes may have limited mobility.
- Skill Dependency: Effective use often requires good aim, timing, and positioning.
- Mana Consumption: Ranged abilities can be mana-intensive.
- Counterplay: Enemies can use various strategies to counter ranged attacks.

### Additional Considerations

- Ranged Attack Speed: The speed at which ranged attacks are launched can significantly impact their effectiveness.
- Ranged Attack Range: The distance at which ranged attacks can be launched is another important factor.
- Ranged Attack Damage: The amount of damage dealt by ranged attacks can vary widely.
- Ranged Attack Effects: Some ranged attacks can have additional effects, such as slowing enemies or applying debuffs.
- Ranged Attack Synergy: Ranged attacks can be combined with other abilities to create powerful combos.
- Ranged Attack Counterplay: Understanding the strengths and weaknesses of ranged attacks is essential for both ranged heroes and their opponents.
