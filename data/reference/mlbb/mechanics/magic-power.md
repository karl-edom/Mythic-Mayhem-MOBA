# Magic power

- For its physical counterpart, see Physical attack.
Magic power (formerly known as magic attack) is an attribute that determines the output of a basic attack or skill. Typically, magic power is used to calculate magic damage from skills. All units have no base and extra magic power, but it can still be increased through talents, equipment, and hero skills.
