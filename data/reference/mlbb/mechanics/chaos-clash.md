# Chaos Clash

Chaos Clash is a limited-time game mode where instead of the battle taking place an ordinary 5v5 setting, it consists of three players that are split into six teams. Unlike ordinary battles, objectives such as turrets, creeps and minions are no longer available. In this mode, there is also no instance of gaining Gold or EXP through eliminating heroes. Instead, they level up and gain Gold and EXP after each round. It also consists of the Annihilation Tide which shrinks after going through four waves.

## Chaos Fragments

After each round, players will get a set of Chaos Fragments. Each player will get two Chaos Fragments and they can be refreshed up to twice. Each fragment is divided into four categories: Buff Fragments (green), Power Fragments (blue), Prime Fragments (purple) and Awakening Fragments (gold).
