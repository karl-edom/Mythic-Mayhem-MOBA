The Land of Dawn is the world that serves as the main setting of Mobile Legends: Bang Bang.

The continent appears in the established Mobile Legends media, including the main game, Mobile Legends Bang Bang, and its spin-offs, Mobile Legends: Adventure and the animated series Legends of Dawn: The Sacred Stone, and the upcoming second animated series made by Base FX and The Little Black Book Studios.

It is where most of the legends and heroes lived, and where many battles have taken place throughout history. The eternal war between light and dark is a well-known example.

## Trivia

- Most of the Regions of the Land of Dawn may be based on real-life continents.
  - The Moniyan Empire, Azrya Woodlands and Northern Vale are based on Europe. Moniyan Empire is based on most cultures of Europe, while Northern Vale is based on Scandinavia or Nordic Countries and Azrya Woodlands is based on Germanic Myths.
  - Cadia Riverlands, Vonetis Sea and half of Agelta Drylands are based on Asia. Cadia Riverlands is mostly based on East Asia while Vonetis Sea are based on Southeast Asia and Half of Agelta Drylands is based on the Middle East
  - Half of Agelta Drylands is based the Americas.
- Northern Vale was previously called Nost Gal (means off-life).
- Moniyan Empire's old name was Mossenia, the other name is Mokenya.
- The first revision of the map debuted on MOONTON EPICON 2019.
