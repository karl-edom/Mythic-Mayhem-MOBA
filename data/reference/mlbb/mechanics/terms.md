# Terms

## Attribute Guide

- **Lifesteal**
- An attribute that restores HP according to damage dealt with Basic Attacks.
- **Spell Vamp**
- An attribute that restores HP according to damage dealt with Skills.
- **Hybrid Lifesteal**
- Lifesteal (Basic Attacks) + Spell Vamp (Skills).
- **Physical Defense**
- An attribute that reduces Physical Damage received.
- **Magic Defense**
- An attribute that reduces Magic Damage received.
- **Physical Penetration**
- An attribute that reduces enemies' Physical Defense.
- **Magic Penetration**
- An attribute that reduces enemies' Magic Defense.
- **Mana Regen**
- The rate at which a hero regenerates Mana.
- **HP Regen**
- The rate at which a hero regenerates HP.
- **Crit Chance**
- Chances to deal multiple Damage with Basic Attacks.
- **Crit Damage**
- The ratio of Crit Damage to Base Damage. The default is 200%.
- **Cooldown Reduction**
- An attribute that reduces the cooldown of hero skills. Usually capped at 40%.
- **Resilience**
- An attribute that reduces the duration of Crowd Control effects inflicted on the hero (not including Suppression, Knock Airborne and Displacement).
- **Healing Effect**
- An attribute that affects the amount of Healing and Shields generated by the hero. The default is 100%.
- **Healing Received**
- An attribute that affects the amount of Healing and Shields received by the hero. The default is 100%.

## State Guide

- **Disarm**
- Low level Crowd Control: Prevents a target from using Basic Attacks. Cannot interrupt skills.
- **Restrain**
- Low level Crowd Control: Prevents a target from leaving a specific area. Cannot interrupt skills.
- **Root**
- Low level Crowd Control: Prevents a target from using Blink/Charge skills (excluding targeted Enhanced Basic Attacks with such effects). Cannot interrupt skills.
- **Immobilize**
- Low level Crowd Control: Prevents a target from moving or using Blink/Charge skills. Cannot interrupt skills.
- **Stun**
- Basic level Crowd Control: Prevents a target from moving, and using Basic Attacks and skills. Can interrupt some skills.
- **Silence**
- Basic level Crowd Control: Prevents a target from using skills or Battle Spells (Purify excluded). Can interrupt some skills.
- **Terrify**
- Basic level Crowd Control: Prevents a target from using Basic Attacks and skills, and causes them to move in random directions. Can interrupt some skills.
- **Taunt**
- Basic level Crowd Control: Forces a target to use Basic Attacks on the caster and chase them, and prevents them from using skills. Can interrupt some skills.
- **Freeze**
- High level Crowd Control: Prevents a target from moving, and using Basic Attacks and skills. Can interrupt a large number of skills.
- **Petrify**
- High level Crowd Control: Prevents a target from moving, and using Basic Attacks and skills. Can interrupt a large number of skills.
- **Airborne**
- High level Crowd Control: Prevents a target from moving. and using Basic Attacks and skills. Can interrupt a large number of skills, and Blink/Charge effects.
- **Transform**
- High level Crowd Control: Prevents a target from using Basic Attacks and skills, and transforms the target into a critter. Can interrupt a large number of skills.
- **Suppress**
- Ultimate level Crowd Control: Prevents a target from moving, and using Basic Attacks and skills. Can interrupt a large number of skills, and Blink/Charge effects. Cannot be removed.
- **Blink**
- Movement ability that can pass through obstacles.
- **Charge**
- Movement ability that cannot pass through obstacles.
- **Displacement**
- Target Displacement: Can interrupt some skill, Blink/Dash or Charge effects (including but not limited to Knocked Back, Pulled and Gathered).
- **Pull**
- Target Displacement: Cannot interrupt skills or Blink.
- **Camouflage**
- Buff: Gains partial invisibility and cannot be targeted by skills.
- **Conceal**
- Buff: Gains complete invisibility and cannot be targeted by skills.
- **Dodge**
- Buff: Gains immunity to enemy Basic Attack damage and effects.
- **Ignore Obstacles**
- Buff: Can pass through obstacles.
- **Slow Immunity**
- Buff: Gains immunity to enemy Slow effects.
- **Control Immunity**
- Buff: Gains immunity to enemy Control and Slow effects (excluding Suppression).
- **Damage Immunity**
- Buff: Gains immunity to enemy damage and damage effects.
- **Death Immunity**
- Buff: HP cannot drop below 1 (cannot be killed).
- **Untargetable**
- Buff: Gains immunity to enemy Control and damage effects, but doesn't remove any existing effects or damage over time.
- **Invincible**
- Buff: Gains Control Immunity, Damage Immunity, and becomes Untargetable.

## Common Terms

- **Hybrid Attack**
- Physical Attack + Magic Power.
- **Hybrid Defense**
- Physical + Magic Defense.
- **Hybrid Penetration**
- Physical + Magic Penetration.
- **Hybrid Regen**
- HP + Mana Regen.
- **Hybrid Lifesteal**
- Lifesteal (Basic Attacks) + Spell Vamp (Skills).
- **Adaptive Attack**
- Increases Physical Attack by the attribute value if the hero has more extra Physical Attack than extra Magic Power. Increases Magic Power by the attribute value if the hero has more extra Magic Power than extra Physical Attack. (Determined by a hero's damage type if the 2 attributes are equal.)
- **Adaptive Penetration**
- Increases Physical Penetration by the attribute value if the hero has more extra Physical Attack than extra Magic Power. Increases Magic Penetration by the attribute value if the hero has more extra Magic Power than extra Physical Attack. (Determined by a hero's damage type if the 2 attnbutes are equal.)
- **Adaptive Damage**
- Increases Physical Damage by the attribute value if the hero has more extra Physical Attack than extra Magic Power. Increases Magic Damage by the attribute value if the hero has more extra Magic Power than extra Physical Attack. (Determined by a hero's damage type if the 2 attributes are equal.)
- **Gank**
- Roam and ambush enemy heroes.
- **Turtle**
- A unique Creep on the battlefield. Heroes will get special BUFF and Gold after defeating it.
- **Lord**
- A unique Creep on the battlefield. Heroes that successfully defeat it will receive Gold, and the Lord will assist them to attack the enemy base.
- **Emblem**
- A special item that can increase heroes' Attributes on the battlefield.
- **Battle Spell**
- Heroes can use Battle Spells in addition to their own skills in battle.
- **Converted Attribute**
- Heroes' one Attribute will be increased by reducing another one.
- **Creep Buff**
- Heroes gain a special BUFF by killing specific Creeps.
- **Draft Pick**
- Draft Pick unlocks at the Epic rank. In Draft Pick, players need to take turns to ban and choose heroes from the hero pool.
- **Invalid Match**
- During Draft Pick, if a player is inactive for the first three minutes, other players on their side can choose to initiate an early surrender. If successful, the match will be deemed invalid. The inactive player and players teamed up with them will incur a rank penalty, but other players will not.
