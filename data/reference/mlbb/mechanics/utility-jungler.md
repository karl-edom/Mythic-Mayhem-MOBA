# Utility Jungler

The Utility Jungler is a tactical doctrine where instead of focusing on farming and kills, the user instead focuses on taking down objectives and supporting the team.

## Description

In a typical match, a jungler is usually an assassin or a marksman whose main specialty is bursting down single targets and deal a lot of damage. However, there's a problem in this type of junglers and their main weakness is their lack of survivability and weakness to CC.

After the jungling update where true damage scales with max HP, Blacklist International jungler Wise decided to innovate and created a new meta called the utility jungler. Unlike other junglers whose main focus is to kill, Wise instead decided on taking down objectives and support his teammates, taking the brunt of the damage and the mid-laner (Hadji) and gold-laner will instead take the kill.

The utility jungler proved to be very effective. Many teams including from the US and Indonesia praised Wise' thinking, earning him the moniker "Objective Wise". However, it still had flaws and the problem is that it didn't fit many teams' playstyle so they instead had to play junglers on the offensive spectrum.
