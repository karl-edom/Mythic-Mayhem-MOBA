# Macro

For its related, narrower concept, see Micro

Macro is a MOBA concept in Mobile Legends: Bang Bang. In simple terms, when a player performs macro, it means they use skills relating to their hero that has strategic impact to their team and to the map.

## Term

Macro means large.

## Related activities

- Lane cutting
See Lane cutting

- Rotation
  - Rotation makes the difference of different roles in game. Midlaners and Roamers often clear minions quickly to rotate to other lanes to help.
  - Abilities that provide movement speed or Emblems like Agility or Wilderness Blessing gives better rotation.
  - It is advised that Midlaner and Roamer rotate to Turtle to aid Jungler, while Marksman rotates to Mid lane to clear minions.

- Lord Preparation
  - Tanks and Fighters should check nearby bushes to secure vision around the Lord during Lord contest.
  - After contesting Lord, clear nearby minion waves to create pressure and force enemy team to stay within their base.
  - When Lord pushes a lane, players can choose to accompany Lord or pressure another lane and steal enemy objectives.
