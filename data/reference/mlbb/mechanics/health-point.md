# Health point

Health (abbreviated as HP) is an attribute that determines how many hit points a unit has. When a unit's health hits zero, it dies. HP regen (attribute), healing, lifesteal, and to-hp-conversions can all be used to restore health.

Health is represented by a green bar in the heads-up display (HUD) over the unit's head, representing the unit's current and maximum health. Damage reduces a unit's current health directly.
