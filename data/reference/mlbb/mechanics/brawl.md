# Brawl

Brawl is a game mode in which you are unable to choose your heroes freely. Instead each player will be given a choice between two random heroes. Fights in this mode happen only in one lane with two turrets defending the base in both teams respectively. Brawl matches usually end in a much shorter time compared to Classic Mode and Ranked. This type of match is prefered for raising lost credit scores or complete tasks.

To find out more about game modes, visit the Battle Field Guide.

## Main features

- Each player is given a choice between 2 random heroes.
- Players can only buy items when dead or before leaving Altar area on each respawn.
- Altar no longer heals or regenerates mana. All healing effect is halved. Base no longer replenishes itself.
- Each hero might have an additional buff or handicap.

## Starting

Players can chat during the selection phase.

All players begin at Level 4 and are given 2500 starting gold to buy items. Once they leave the Base, they must die to be able to purchase items again.

## In-game quick Guides to Brawl

## Healing Glyph

Heroes who rarely die in battle will drop a healing glyph. (Heals both Max HP and Mana.)

## Turrets

Turrets in Brawl behave similarly as in Classic Games. However turrets are weaker and do not have a golden shield.

The second turret of each side releases a wave that kills all minions upon being destroyed.

## Minions

Gold and experience gained from minions are higher than in Classic Mode.

At the 12 minute mark, Super Minions are deployed to avoid lengthy games.

## Crab

There are two crabs on opposite sides of the lane that spawn at 0:30 and respawn 30 seconds after their death. Killing a Frozen Crab regenerates small amounts of health, gives some golds and a small shield. Nearby allies who help with the crab is also credited with some gold. This crab can be used as a stepping stone for heroes like Lancelot, Joy or Natalia, interact with certain heroes like Vexana, Benedetta or Yi Sun-shin, or check if enemy is in the nearest bush or commit suicide to buy items when health is too low.

## Healing Runes

There are two healing runes on opposite sides of the lane that spawn at 0:30 and respawn 35 seconds after being picked up. The timer and sound effect is visible and audible to everyone. The rune continuously heals the one who picks up during the 5-second delay, then regenerates some health and 40% max mana to all allied heroes in range. If the one who picks up dies during the delay, the healing is canceled.

Sun's clones can copy the animation although only one will take effect.

Natalia under Conceal will also conceal the animation.

Diggie can pick up the rune even while dead.

## Barrel

There is a Barrel on each side that spawns at the 0:20 mark and every 30 seconds after being picked up. Pick this Barrel and shoots towards enemies to catch them off-guard!

The Barrel makes the user untargetable shortly, deals impact True damage, and has a 15-second cooldown, making it impossible to snatch two barrels at once. When someone picks up a Barrel, a sound is played audible to everyone on the map.

The Barrel is also disabled by rooted effect.

A dead Diggie or untargetable hero can pick up the Barrel.

## Matchmaking

Unlike Classic or Ranked, there is no Rank restrictions in Brawl. Meaning you can queue matches that one side is 5 man solo queue while the other is a team of 5, or one side is 5 man Mythic Immortal while the other is someone who just downloaded the game.

Being AFK, avoiding teamfight or verbal abuse is fully punishable through reports.

## Tips & Tricks

- Recommended Team Composition
There are many heroes to choose from with different styles of play. Knowing the heroes' skills, strengths, and weaknesses along with optimal positioning is vital. Prioritize Assassins, Mages or Fighters as they have a lot of damage output and crowd control. Avoid Marksman as they are item-dependent and squishy early on, unless those with good minion clearing and area of effect abilities like Beatrix or Granger. Avoid teams with too many Mages, too many Marksmen or too many Tanks.

- Recommended Battle Spells
  - Flameshot has very low cooldown and can be used to check bush, secure kills or cancel abilities.

  - At least one player should bring these followng items:
    - Revitalize so that their team can resustain after fight as the Altar no longer heals, especially when your team in a bad position that they cannot secure the Healing runes; and
    - Purify, if your hero has good damage output but vulnerable to crowd control (CC).
    - However, Sprint is not very recommended.

- Make use of the crowd
Brawl's unique map can massively enhance heroes such as Luo Yi, Odette, Lancelot, Xavier, Fanny while heroes that rely too much on basic attacks like Freya or Thamuz become useless.

Likewise, you should avoid staying close together when enemies are Atlas or Tigreal.
- Buy the right stuff

Instead of buying a decent item, buying smaller components might give better advantage. You can also wait a few seconds and ping allies that you almost have enough gold for items.j

instead of rushing a 5 man team with full Sky Piercer, item that gives healing reduction can greatly counter the enemy team.

- Hiding in the bush

Take advantage of bushes to ambush enemies, especially suitable for heroes like Helcurt, Hilda, Natalia, Luo Yi. Be careful if enemies have such heroes.

- Replay hero selection

At the beginning, players will be given a choice between 2 heroes. If no heroes are very unsuitable for you to play, you roll a dice to get a new hero and send your discarded hero to the pool for others. Sometimes only one hero discarded is enough for everyone to swap. You can chat and ask other players or use quick chats and emotes. Keep in mind that if the hero is discarded twice and disappear from everyone's selection, it might reappear again if you use another dice, which will waste your dice. Up to 10 heroes can appear at the discarded slot.

Some heroes are difficult for you to play, but it does not mean it is difficult for your allies, swap them out and avoid hoarding two good heroes while your allies having none.

- Enlist the enemy

Since it is only one lane, it is easier to guess your opponents' movements and place your hero in the best position to either defend or attack.

When the enemy team has Xavier, Novaria, expect that you will be damaged or revealed while buying items at Altar or reaching the battlefield.

You can check up on enemies's items to see if they left their Altar or not.

- Use natural objects
You can save up a Barrel to escape after diving in, turret dive or use it to shorten the distance with Flicker.
Barrels first spawn at 20 seconds, while the first Healing Runes are at 30 seconds, it is best to secure the bush on the right and the glyph close to it, so that your team might at least disable a healing option after fight of the enemies. Use the barrel to stop the enemies when they try to secure the Healing Rune.

## Emblems

Seasoned Hunter is replaced with another emblem.

Wilderness Blessing's 10% bonus movement speed is applied to the entire map.

## Battle Spells

All heroes are given Flicker by default alongside another chosen battle spell.

Arrival, Recall and Retribution are unavailable by default. Roaming and Jungling equipments are also disabled.

All battle spells begin with 50% of their normal cooldown.
