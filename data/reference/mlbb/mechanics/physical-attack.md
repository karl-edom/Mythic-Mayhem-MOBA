# Physical attack

- For its magical counterpart, see Magic power.
Physical attack (physical ATK) is an attribute that determines the output of a basic attack or skill. Typically, physical attack is used to calculate physical damage. All units have base physical attack and 0 extra physical attack, but it can be increased through talents, equipment, and hero skills.

## Trivia

- The highest known physical attack that can be ever obtained is 2139. This can be done by using Level 15 Valentina equipped with a skin, Level 60 Mage Emblem, the Thrill and Weapon Master talents, one Blade of Despair, five Holy Crystals, the Magic Potion when she copies a physical damage hero using her Ultimate.
