# Map

Map is a battlefield area where players playing heroes and battle against each other in order to win the game. There are different maps that will depends on game mode. Some maps have different variants based on seasonal events. Each map will be based on the Land of Dawn locations except for Evolve Mode.

## Overview

### Lanes

There are 3 lanes on the map: Top lane, Middle Lane (aka Midlane), and Bottom lane (aka Bot lane).

Top lane and Bot Lane is usually got switched, and sometimes both Gold lane and EXP lane whether in Top Lane or Bot Lane. There are signs at the corners of the map to indicate which role for which lane. Symbol  is indicate Gold Lane and usually for Marksman role (they rely on basic attack and need to get items as quick as possible), while  is indicate EXP Lane and is best for Fighter role (they rely on skills). Middle lane is fit for Mage position (make them easier to ganking to Top lane or Bot lane). There are also jungle area; an area between lanes, is fit for Jungler role. For Tank or Support, they always roaming from one lane to another to provide vision for their team.
