# Adaptive attributes

- For its counterpart, see Hybrid attributes.
Adaptive attributes are the attributes that adapt its value depends on the hero's attack power and damage type.

## Adaptive Attack

> Increases Physical Attack by the attribute value if the hero has more extra Physical Attack than extra Magic Power. Increases Magic Power by the attribute value if the hero has more extra Magic Power than extra Physical Attack. (Determined by a hero's damage type if the 2 attributes are equal) — MLBB Common Terms
