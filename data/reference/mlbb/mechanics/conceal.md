# Conceal

- For the roaming boots, see Boots § Conceal.
> Enter Stealth State within the duration, won't be easily spotted visually by the enemy, can't be targeted by skills. — MLBB Skill Terms

Conceal (noun concealment) or stealth is a gameplay mechanic that makes a unit hidden from enemy vision for a determined period of time. If it is active, they will be hidden from the mini-map and cannot be targeted by single-target attacks, cannot be automatically selected by single-target abilities. It also cancels any ongoing and cannot be locked by Manual Targeting or Hero Lock Mode. Heroes can be seen with visual cues or distortions, or cannot be detected at all.

A conceal state may end if the unit either deals or takes damage. It can be negated through skills that reveal heroes.

## Types of conceal

### Camouflage

> Camouflage — MLBB Skill Terms
Camouflage is a type of conceal in which a unit blends into the battlefield. A camouflage unit can be detected through "distortions of their surroundings" as a visual clue.

- Does not disjoin incoming projectiles. For examples, Beatrix's Nibiru launches a barrage of attacks homing to Lesley who camouflages amid, the subsequent attacks still hit and breaks her camouflages.

- Single-target abilities, such as Lunox's Chaos Assault will not automatically lock on the target, but can be aimed to hit them.

- Abilities that lock onto all enemies in range, such as Lunox's Starlight Pulse stil home to nearby Camouflaged enemies.

- A unit, being exposed by reveal sources, enter Camouflage state will retain the bonus that comes with it, excluding the Camouflage.
  - Natalia hit by Granger's Death Sonata is exposed irregardless if Natalia conceals before or afterwards, but the Enhanced basic attack is not removed.

- When a camouflaged unit is in the same bush with an enemy, or with an enemy's trap or sources that provide vision but does not expose them, their camouflage state is known to enemies.

### Half-stealth

> Enter Half-Stealth State within the duration, won't be easily spotted visually by the enemy but can be targeted by skills. — MLBB Skill Terms
Half-stealth is a type of conceal that makes a unit became translucent for a period of time. If this is active, the hero cannot be seen visually but can be targeted with single-target attacks. This stealth will end if the hero deals or takes damage. Only Ling can trigger this effect with Finch Poise.

### Invisible

> Invisible — MLBB Skill Terms
Invisible is a type of conceal in which a unit is completely unseen to the enemy's sight and its.

- Does not disjoin incoming projectiles. For examples, Beatrix's Nibiru launches a barrage of attacks homing to Miya who turns invisible amid, the subsequent attacks still hit and breaks her camouflages. Therefore these projectiles allow tracking the direction enemies might possibly go.

- Abilities that lock onto all enemies in range, such as Lunox's Starlight Pulse still home to nearby Camouflaged enemies.

## Negating conceal

With the exception of Miya's Hidden Moonlight and Guinevere's Spatial Migration, most conceal states end when damaged. It is recommended to use AoE (area of effect) damage abilities, consistent damage or ideally paired with crowd controls, to force opponents out of this state.

Heroes who are affected by the sight debuff have their location directly revealed, rendering conceal useless. It is recommended to use heroes that reveal opponents with their skills.
