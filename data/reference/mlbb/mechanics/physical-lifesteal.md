# Physical lifesteal

> An attribute to regenerate HP according to Physical Damage of Basic Attacks.
Physical lifesteal was an attribute that restores health equal to a percentage when dealing physical damage through basic attacks.

Since Patch 1.7.94 went live on June 26 2023, both physical lifesteal and magic lifesteal were removed and succeeded by the lifesteal attribute.

## Characteristics of physical lifesteal

Physical lifesteal starts at 0%, but is increased by emblems, equipment, or hero skills.

Unless otherwise stated, physical lifesteal was limited to basic attacks that deals physical damage.

Physical lifesteal's healing is multiplied by a unit's healing received but not by its healing effect.

## Increasing physical lifesteal

### Hero abilities

- Alucard's Fission Wave (60%)
- Aulus's Undying Fury (15%)
- Hanabi's Ninjutsu: Petal Barrage (8% + (2% x skill level); up to 18%)
- Masha's Ancient Strength (After losing the first HP Bar, gains 25% physical lifesteal, and gains extra 15% physical lifesteal after losing the second HP Bar; A total of 40% physical lifesteal.)
- Ruby's Let's Dance! (10%)

### Emblems

- Marksman Emblem (5.00%)
  - Greed (1.50%/3.00%/5.00%)

### Equipment

- Demon Hunter Sword (3% per each Basic Attack hit, stacks up to 15%)
- Endless Battle (10%)
- Haas' Claws (20%, gains extra 15% physical lifesteal when below 50% HP; 40% physical lifesteal in total)
- Vampire Mallet (8%)
- Wind of Nature (10%)
- Rose Gold Meteor (10%)
