## Description

They are duo creatures in the jungle, these duo jungle monster will give more gold than the smaller monsters and will give a Purple Buff (also known as Blue Buff.) They are located in the left side of the jungle area.

After Thunder Fenrirs are killed, a Hero's Path will open behind them and last until they respawn. You can pass through it as a shortcut.

## Story

### Sanctum Island

Thunder Fenrirs are powerful thunder beasts that dwell in the ruins of the temple garden built by the Ancient Ones. They take great care of their cubs, showing wisdom and maternal love rarely seen on other thunder beasts.

### Imperial Sanctuary

In the Imperial Sanctuary, any creature could be a lethal threat. Take Serpent, the most mysterious and sneaky one, as an example - for a long time, Serpents have been the challengers' favorite. Roaming beside the river, Serpents love to attack the intrudes who break into their territory. They often go in an overwhelming quantity, thus, making with challengers struggle. Anyone who has the courage and strength to beat a Serpent will be rewarded with endless strength, which can be obtained from its blood.

## Appearance

name: Thunder Fenrir has modification appearances of the wolf. In Imperial Sanctuary map, these creatures named name: Serpent. Also known as name: Statued Shocker and name: Statued Chaser in Celestial Palace and Western Expanse maps.

## Tips

Thunder Fenrir has a spawn time of 45 seconds, with a further cooldown of 90 seconds. Also, it is relatively easy to kill, unlike the Turtle. However, you must slay the smaller one first, as it is faster in attack speed but low on HP. Killing this duo will give you buffs. These buffs consist of 10% cooldown reduction, 60% reduced Mana consumption from your skills, and a plus 20% energy consumption bonus. These buffs are really good for heroes, who need to spam their skills.

## History

When this creep was first released, they were known as the name: Spinner and name: Spider.

The Spinner and Spider pair used to be a single, large Spider  (before it was renamed to the large Spinner, with its little Spider pair member). The little Spider creep seems to be the replacement for a nearby jungle creep whose position (located immediately left of the mid-lane) has never been filled since.

This Jungle Creep used its fists for punching. Their backhands seem to have white plates with blue spiral 'G-like' runes. Their eyes glowed bluish too. The white beard would seem to be tinted by the glow of these blue runes and bright eyes. These creeps appeared muscly and perhaps rock-like, though their body material is not yet known. They seemed bald. When attacked, their arms would rise outstretched to each side at shoulder height while charging in to combat the Heroes. When at rest, their arms were slightly lower but still kept a little outwards. Upon defeat, their upper body would tilt to one side and fling one arm upwards and the other arm slumped down before stabilizing and swapping the directions of its arms, collapsing backwards with the previously slumped arm flung upwards.
