## Description

Heroes will get the Power of Cyclone when going through here that makes them glide forward after a brief stay of 1.2s. During gliding, Heroes can pass through some obstacles. The Power of Cyclone will disappear once it is activated and appear again when gathering enough power. There are 4 Cyclone Eyes on the battlefield in total. The Cooldown is 30s and it refreshes at the 40s of the game, with the availability is indicated on minimap when it is sight.

## Battlefield Effects

The Cyclone Eye has been one of many Battlefield Effects implemented in Rank Mode:

- Eternal Eye [6/22-6/23]
Greatly reduced the respawn time of Cyclone Eye at Jungle Area: 45s >> 5s.

- Elemental Eyes [Golden Month event]
  - Upon respawn, the eyes with their element are visible on minimap. Every 90 seconds, 4 Cyclone Eyes are respawned at Jungle Area. Pick them up will grant special effects.
  - Power of Lightning: damaging enemies will paralyze them, reduce their damage and movement speed by 10% for 3 seconds.
  - Power of Burning: damaging enemies will deal an additional 2% enemy's max health as damage over 3 seconds (cooldown: 5 seconds)
  - Power of Tide: restore 2% max HP per second, taking damage cancels the effect.
  - Power of Gale: +15% movement speed boost
  - Blessing of Gold: instantly gain 50 gold then 10 gold per second for a short while. You also gain 5% Max HP, Adaptive Hybrid Regen, Hybrid Defense for 60 seconds.
    - Your hero model also enlarges for the duration.

## Story

The Cyclone Eye has been in existence when the Imperial Sanctuary was established. Just like an oracle claimed, they were the ancient arcane left by the King of Light, containing the great power of cyclone. Only a true warrior could master this ancient power and make use of it, otherwise, those who step on it will be lost forever in the cyclone.

## Mechanics

- The effect of Cyclone Eye fully triggers Lancelot's passive Soul Cutter, Phoveus's passive Demonic Force and blocked by Khufra's Bouncing Ball but is not disabled by Minsitthar's King's Calling

- The effect of Cyclone Eye is considered a displacement, and does not interrupt skills, such as Pharsa's Feathered Air Strike

- When the effect of Cyclone Eye takes effect, it can be nullified by casting dash abilities (such as Yi Sun-shin's Traceless) at the same time.

- The Cyclone Eye can grant the Power of Cyclone for multiple heroes all at once regardless of factions, if they all step the cyclone at a nearly same time.

- The gliding distance extends when there is an obstacle in the expected landing point.
